Editorial findings:

- SMDI has five distinct sub-schemes under a three-year FY 2024-25 to FY 2026-27 framework with a total outlay of INR 500 crore; they are separate records because their applicants, outputs and benefits differ.
- Common facilities supports cluster/testing infrastructure; MIS RID supports key components/raw materials/accessories; capacity-building supports eligible courses; clinical studies supports evidence generation; promotion supports eligible sector activities.
- The official launch release describes common/testing grants up to INR 20 crore/INR 5 crore, MIS capital subsidy of 10%–20% capped at INR 10 crore per project, and course-support amounts; each remains subject to the detailed guidelines.
- The current Department page lists 2025/2026 modifications and 2026 calls. The records therefore preserve call and applicant uncertainty rather than silently marking any route active.
- No record is publishable until the latest notice, component-specific eligibility, deadline, PMA route, evidence requirements and any amendment are manually reviewed.
