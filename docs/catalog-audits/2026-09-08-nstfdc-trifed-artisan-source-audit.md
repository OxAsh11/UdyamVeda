## Reconciliation recorded

- The NSTFDC manual describes concessional finance for project-related assets and working capital for tribal artisans empanelled with TRIFED.
- It states up to INR 50,000 for an individual and up to INR 5 lakh per SHG/cooperative society. Individual and group ceilings remain separate.
- Its lending table states NSTFDC share up to 90% of unit cost, with SCA rates of 2% for women and 3% for men and beneficiary rates of 4% for women and 6% for men.
- TRIFED's current page describes the empanelment route and states that groups/organisations must contain at least 70% tribal persons, with key positions held by ST persons.
- The cited sources do not establish a nationwide deadline, repayment schedule, contribution base, security/collateral rule, processing fee or complete SCA document list.
- The route must be presented as two linked but separate checks: TRIFED empanelment/market access and NSTFDC/SCA credit appraisal.
