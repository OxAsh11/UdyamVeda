## Controlled interpretations

### DSIR PRISM

PRISM is modeled as track-specific innovation and technology-development support for individuals, startups, MSMEs, and eligible institutions. The cluster guideline's project and support values are kept separate from individual/startup tracks. The record is closed/review-gated because the earlier window and the later Creative India/PRISM evidence must be reconciled before a new call or successor route can be asserted.

### DCIS

DCIS is modeled as a Department of Telecommunications innovation-support route for startups and MSMEs in digital communications. The official portal and revised guideline describe historical application rounds and an extension boundary through March 2026; the record is therefore closed until a later extension or fresh call is verified. Any grant ceiling remains separate from the scheme's overall allocation and is not treated as an automatic entitlement.

### Telecom PLI

Telecom PLI is performance-linked manufacturing support, not a grant or loan. The record requires an Indian company, target-segment mapping, investment/sales evidence, approval, and a currently authorized application route. A scheme guideline or application acknowledgement does not establish incentive entitlement, and the application-window condition remains explicit.

### CEGSSC

CEGSSC is modeled as a credit-enhancement guarantee supporting lender facilities for eligible Scheduled Caste-owned businesses. It is not a direct grant, direct loan, or guaranteed sanction. Guarantee coverage, ownership/control conditions, lender route, tenure, and current portal terms require reconciliation before publication.

## Human review gate

All four records remain `in_review` and publication-blocked. A reviewer must confirm the latest amendment, open/closed status, applicant definitions, financial instrument, ceilings, qualifying evidence, application route, reporting, recovery/clawback, and source anchors before any public recommendation is enabled.
