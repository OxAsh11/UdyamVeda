# AgriSURE source audit

Date: 9 September 2026

## Finding

The Startup India June 2026 Playbook lists AgriSURE as an equity-investment support route for agriculture and rural startups. The primary operational source is the official NABVENTURES fund-manager page, cross-checked with the official NABARD FAQ and fraud-prevention notice and the Ministry of Agriculture/PIB launch release.

AgriSURE is not one direct founder grant. It has two distinct routes: a Fund-of-Funds scheme for eligible Category I/II AIFs and a Direct Scheme for early-stage agriculture/rural startups. The public materials describe fund-level corpus buckets of INR 450 crore and INR 300 crore, totalling INR 750 crore. Those figures are not individual applicant entitlements.

## Catalog treatment

- Structured record: `agrisure-agri-fund-startups-rural-enterprises`
- Status: `paused` / `in_review`
- Benefits: direct equity, FoF AIF contribution, permitted equity-related instruments and permitted debt instruments kept in separate buckets
- Startup and AIF eligibility routes kept separate
- Direct contact route: NABVENTURES / agrisure@nabard.org
- No fee for seeking assistance, based on the official NABARD public notice
- Investment remains merit-based and fund-document-controlled
- Playbook lead is resolved, but publication remains blocked pending human source, eligibility, window/fine-print, amendment and factual review
