# BIRAC LEAP Fund source audit — 2026-09-09

## Decision

Create a paused, review-gated structured draft for BIRAC's Launching Entrepreneurial Driven Affordable Products (LEAP) Fund. Do not publish it as a universally open startup opportunity.

## Authority and evidence

- BIRAC is the implementing agency under the Department of Biotechnology.
- The official BIRAC guideline establishes the incubator framework, Indian-ownership threshold, startup investment ceiling, equity/equity-linked instruments, SEED dependency, non-concurrency, governance, reporting and exit provisions.
- The current BIRAC page lists six LEAP partner BioNEST incubators and a startup-facing partner route, but its displayed impact figures are marked February 2023.
- The Startup India Playbook is discovery and cross-check evidence; BIRAC controls the operational record.

## Fine-print findings

- The startup benefit is up to INR 1 crore in equity or equity-linked instruments, not a non-repayable grant.
- The incubator-level up-to-INR 5 crore grant-in-aid and startup-level up-to-INR 1 crore investment ceiling are separate value buckets.
- Startups suitable for up to INR 30 lakh belong under SEED; LEAP cannot be treated as an additive second tranche.
- SEED, LEAP and AcE are mutually exclusive instruments and SEED/LEAP support must not be concurrent. LEAP also depends on completion and non-termination of SEED support.
- The incubator selects startups and controls partner-level intake, valuation, dilution, instrument terms and investment approval.
- No universal current startup call, deadline or application form was verified.

## Publication guard

The record remains `paused`, its version remains `in_review`, and a reviewer must verify the current selected partner list, partner-level intake, fund availability, SEED status, instrument/valuation/dilution terms, diligence, IP, governance, amendments and current application route before publication.
