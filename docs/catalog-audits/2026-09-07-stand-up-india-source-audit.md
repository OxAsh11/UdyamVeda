# Stand Up India source audit — 7 September 2026

Status: `in_review`; historical/closed opportunity, not publishable as an active loan.

## Official sources checked

- Department of Financial Services scheme page: https://financialservices.gov.in/stand-india-scheme-supi
- Historical handholding route: https://www.standupmitra.in/
- National credit-linked route: https://www.jansamarth.in/

## Reconciled facts

- The DFS page describes the historical scheme as a composite loan of Rs 10 lakh to Rs 1 crore for eligible women and SC/ST entrepreneurs above 18 setting up greenfield manufacturing, services, trading or eligible allied-agriculture activity.
- It states repayment up to seven years including a moratorium of up to 18 months, margin money up to 15%, and minimum own contribution of 10%.
- Most importantly, the current page says the scheme ran up to 31 March 2025 and separately describes a Budget 2025-26 successor proposal whose EFC note was still under preparation.
- The record therefore uses a `closed` window. A successor is not merged into this record and must receive its own official notification, eligibility, window and application record.

## Publication blockers

- Reviewer must confirm whether a formal successor notification has replaced or extended the historical scheme.
- Bank appraisal, interest, security, margin, repayment and sanction remain lender-specific.
- StandUpMitra/JanSamarth availability must not be treated as proof that the closed scheme is accepting applications.

## Decision

Keep the record in review with a closed historical window. Do not recommend it as an active application opportunity.
