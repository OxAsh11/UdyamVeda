# DST NIDHI-EIR and Technology Business Incubator source audit

Audit date: 08 September 2026

## Official evidence inspected

- [NIDHI Entrepreneur-in-Residence programme page](https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/nidhieir/), the official Department of Science and Technology programme source.
- [Startup India incubator schemes page](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/incubator-schemes.html), used as a discovery source for the DST Technology Business Incubator route.
- [NIDHI documents index](https://nidhi.dst.gov.in/documents/) and the linked NIDHI-EIR guideline route for centre lists, programme documents, and formal eligibility terms.

## NIDHI Entrepreneur-in-Residence (EIR)

- The record models EIR as an incubator-mediated fellowship for Indian students, researchers, innovators, and aspiring entrepreneurs pursuing a technology business idea.
- The current programme page describes monthly support of INR 10,000 to INR 30,000 for up to 12 months, alongside TBI infrastructure, mentoring, technical/financial advice, and industry connections.
- The record keeps the fellowship separate from a startup grant, investment, loan, or combined eligible-value calculation.
- Applicants must use a selected NIDHI-EIR centre/TBI route. The central programme page is not itself proof of a current nationwide call or selection.
- The guideline route and programme page require reconciliation because the guideline describes a conditional extension beyond 12 months while the current page states a 12-month maximum.

## DST Technology Business Incubator (TBI)

- The Startup India page describes institution-led incubation support for technology and knowledge-based startups, including mentoring and legal, financial, technical, and intellectual-property support.
- The applicant is the eligible host institution or incubator proposal, not an individual startup seeking a direct founder grant.
- The discovery page is not sufficient to establish the current DST funding pattern, eligible costs, proposal window, or amount. Those fields remain unknown until the underlying DST authority material is checked.

## Reviewer decision

Keep both records `in_review` and publication-blocked. Before publication, verify the current NIDHI-EIR centre list and call, formal education/experience rules, full-time and concurrent-support restrictions, TBI selection terms, the 12/18-month duration conflict, and any current DST TBI proposal window and funding conditions. Do not show either incubator-mediated route as a universal open grant.

## Source anchors

- NIDHI-EIR: programme description, support amount and duration, selected-centre application route, and beneficiary sections.
- DST TBI: `Schemes for Incubators; DST - TBI; Objective; Eligibility criteria` on the Startup India discovery page.
