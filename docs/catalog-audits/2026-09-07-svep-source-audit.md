# SVEP source audit — 7 September 2026

Status: `in_review`; rolling programme support subject to local State/block coverage.

## Official sources checked

- [SVEP official portal](https://www.svep.nrlm.gov.in/landing)
- [SVEP FAQ](https://svep.nrlm.gov.in/svepdemo/pdf/FAQ_SVEP.pdf)
- [SVEP contact route](https://svep.nrlm.gov.in/contactUs)
- [Ministry of Rural Development](https://rural.gov.in/)

## Reconciled facts

- The official portal describes SVEP as part of DAY-NRLM and as a centrally sponsored programme implemented since June 2011; it records the sub-scheme approval and guideline history.
- The programme focuses on rural self-employment and early-stage enterprises, with business-development support, training, mentoring, finance access and convergence guidance.
- The FAQ describes an authorised profile and business-plan workflow involving local implementation actors such as CRP-EPs and enterprise-support structures.
- The current portal records an update on 19 January 2026.

## Publication blockers

- Confirm whether the applicant’s State/block is currently covered and identify the authorised local route.
- Confirm current finance mechanism, contribution, repayment, eligible activities, documents, service timeline and local intake.
- Do not present SVEP as a universal direct grant or national loan ceiling.

## Decision

Keep as a rolling, review-gated business-support opportunity with finance, training, mentoring and convergence as separate benefit buckets.
