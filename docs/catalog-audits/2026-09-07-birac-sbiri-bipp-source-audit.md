Editorial findings:

- SBIRI and BIPP are separate records. SBIRI is the early-stage small-business route; BIPP is the advanced, high-risk, transformational route that generally targets projects ending at TRL 7 or above.
- SBIRI funding depends on startup age, prior BIRAC grant history and the applicant/entity budget. The first-time-startup contribution cap is not a total project-cost cap.
- BIPP assistance, applicant contribution, milestone release and royalty obligations must be displayed separately. Category IV facility support has separate conditions and must not be represented as a generic founder grant.
- The older SBIRI webpage contains soft-loan language alongside the newer 2025 guideline. The record intentionally preserves that conflict and blocks publication until a reviewer reconciles the current funding route.
- Recurring call windows are recorded as evidence-backed but still require confirmation against the active call notice before a deadline is shown to users.
