# PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana source audit — 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit keeps the two PMVBRY benefit routes separate: Part A is an employee first-timer incentive, while Part B is an employer incentive for additional employment. They must not be shown as one combined grant or as a universal business subsidy.

## Official evidence reviewed

- EPFO/Ministry of Labour and Employment PMVBRY Guidelines: https://pmvbry.epfindia.gov.in/wp-content/themes/epfo-child/assets/images/PMVBRY-Final.pdf
- PMVBRY official portal: https://pmvbry.epfindia.gov.in/

## Evidence interpretation

- The guideline gives an effective date of 1 August 2025 and a registration period ending 31 July 2027.
- Part A supports eligible first-time employees, subject to wage, EPFO/ECR, UAN authentication, continuity, instalment and financial-literacy conditions; its ceiling is ₹15,000 per first timer.
- Part B supports eligible establishments that generate net additional employment above a baseline and threshold. It uses employee wage slabs, ECR filing, contribution and establishment conditions, with additional duration for manufacturing.
- The guideline includes cessation, fraud/inquiry exclusions, DBT payment, baseline calculations and separate establishment-versus-employee payment routes.

## Human review gate

The two records remain `in_review` and publication-blocked until a reviewer verifies the current EPFO portal workflow, registration and ECR requirements, baseline dates, wage interpretation, UAN authentication, manufacturing duration, payment timing, amendment history and whether the scheme remains open for the relevant user profile.
