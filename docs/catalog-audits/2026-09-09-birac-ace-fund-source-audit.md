## Editorial decisions

AcE is encoded as an indirect equity-investment opportunity, not as a direct grant, loan or universal startup application. The BIRAC commitment to a daughter fund, the partner's 2x investment obligation and the up-to-INR 7 crore portfolio-entity boundary remain separate value buckets. The current record is `paused` because a current partner-level startup intake was not verified.
