## Reconciled facts

- The official FAQ describes DCIS as support for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market entry and commercialization.
- The FAQ displays ceilings of up to INR 50 lakh for startups, INR 2 crore for MSMEs and up to INR 10 crore for higher-funding technology. These are track-level ceilings, not automatic applicant awards.
- The DoT guideline identifies TCOE India as implementing agency, requires technical due diligence and milestone-linked support, and distinguishes eligible R&D/prototyping/testing/piloting costs from excluded administrative and infrastructure costs.
- The guideline states a four-year duration for 2022-23 to 2025-26. The portal describes invited rounds in 2021, 2022 and 2023, while the FAQ says online calls are hosted on an ongoing basis. The current application window therefore remains unresolved.

## Publication blockers

- Verify any post-March-2026 extension or current open call.
- Confirm current entity, ownership, project-stage, technical, financial and document requirements.
- Reconcile the grant, debt/convertible and milestone/tranche routes from the operative call and grant agreement.
- Do not present programme ceilings as guaranteed individual benefits.

## Decision

Keep the existing DCIS record review-gated and link the Playbook lead to it; do not create a duplicate opportunity.
