# Assistance to Training Institutions (ATI) source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers ATI under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. It distinguishes institution-level infrastructure/programme support from founder and trainee-facing discovery.

## Official evidence reviewed

- ATI official portal: https://ati.msme.gov.in/ATI/home.aspx
- ATI information surface: https://ati.msme.gov.in/Index2.aspx
- Revised ATI guideline circular dated 25 April 2025: https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/ATIguidelinesEnglish.pdf
- MyMSME ATI description: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsmeMob/MsmeScheme/Pages/0_3_1.html
- ATI trainee registration: https://ati.msme.gov.in/potentialtrainees/TraineesRegistration.aspx
- ATI approved programme search: https://ati.msme.gov.in/PotentialTrainees/ViewApprovedProgramme.aspx
- ATI course/module catalogue: https://ati.msme.gov.in/ModuleHome.aspx

## Evidence interpretation

- ATI is institution-led. The current portal describes capital support for training infrastructure and programme support for entrepreneurship and skill development.
- The 25 April 2025 circular expands the infrastructure route to existing State-level EDIs and specified State Government-owned training institutions, including DICs in the Eastern Region and NER.
- The portal separately supports approved-programme discovery, trainee registration, course/module browsing and apex/partner institution navigation. These are different from an institution's capital-grant proposal.
- MyMSME's displayed INR 150 lakh/50 percent and regional/per-trainee terms are retained as historical context only. The 2025 revised boundary and current operative terms require reconciliation before any amount is treated as current.
- Portal trainee/programme counts are programme reporting, not applicant entitlement.
