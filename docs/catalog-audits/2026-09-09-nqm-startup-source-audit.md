# National Quantum Mission startup-support source audit

Date: 9 September 2026

## Finding

The Startup India Playbook lead NQM resolves to a genuine DST startup-support route. DST's official guidelines explicitly cover DPIIT-registered Indian startups developing scalable, market-fit quantum-technology products or services. Support is delivered through NQM thematic hubs and may include milestone-based financial support, equity/CCPS, mentoring, facilities and user/procurement connections.

NQM is not a single unconditional national grant. The applicant must select the correct quantum vertical and apply to only one current hub route. Funding slabs, equity/CCPS terms, valuation, dilution, milestones and current windows remain call- and agreement-controlled.

## Catalog treatment

- Structured record: `national-quantum-mission-startup-support`
- Status: `paused` / `in_review`
- Authority: Department of Science and Technology / National Quantum Mission
- Benefits: hub-mediated equity/CCPS or financial support, incubation, mentoring, testing/facility access and possible market/procurement support kept separate
- Application: one current hub route among the four official NQM verticals
- Publication: blocked pending current hub-window, amendment, terms and factual review
