## Current observations

- The official portal states that Udyam registration is free, paperless and based on self-declaration; private intermediaries are not authorized to undertake the registration activity.
- The portal states that no documents or proof need to be uploaded, but the form still requires Aadhaar verification and applicable PAN/GST-linked information.
- The current form separates identity responsibility by entity type: proprietor, managing partner, karta, or organisation/authorised signatory for companies, LLPs, cooperatives, societies and trusts.
- The portal states that one enterprise must not file more than one Udyam Registration and that multiple manufacturing or service activities may be specified in one registration.
- The Ministry of MSME Annual Report 2025-26 records the revised classification thresholds effective 2025-04-01: micro INR 2.5 crore/INR 10 crore, small INR 25 crore/INR 100 crore, and medium INR 125 crore/INR 500 crore for investment/turnover.
- The portal states that the certificate has a dynamic QR code and registration does not require renewal.

## UdyamVeda boundary

UdyamVeda may provide a preparation checklist, explain the official flow, validate non-sensitive readiness and hand off to the official portal. It must not impersonate the applicant, collect OTPs, or store Aadhaar/PAN values by default. Any narrowly scoped identity-data handling requires separate legal and security approval.
