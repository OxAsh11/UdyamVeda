# Public Procurement Policy for MSEs source audit — 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the Public Procurement Policy for Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs) Order, 2012. It is market-access and procurement preference support, not a cash grant or assured order value.

## Official evidence reviewed

- MyMSME Public Procurement Policy portal: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsme/Reg/PPP_Home.aspx
- MyMSME Single Point Registration context: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsmeMob/MsmeScheme/Pages/1_2_3.html
- Ministry of MSME schemes index: https://my.msme.gov.in/mymsme/Scheme.aspx

## Evidence interpretation

- The portal states that the policy is notified under section 11 of the MSMED Act, 2006 and is effective from 1 April 2012.
- It describes the Central Ministry, Department and PSU annual procurement target of 20% from MSEs, with a 4% sub-target within that amount for MSEs owned by SC/ST entrepreneurs.
- Operational support includes free tender sets, exemption from earnest-money payment for registered MSEs, an L1+15% price-band mechanism subject to matching L1 terms, reserved items, procurement plans, and vendor-development programmes.
- The evidence describes registration routes and operational reporting through MSME Sambandh. GeM and Single Point Registration are access routes, not entitlements to funding or procurement.
