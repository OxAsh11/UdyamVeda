# Legal Metrology source audit

Date: 2026-09-07  
Status: review-gated; not approved for publication

## Scope

This batch adds three separate compliance routes under the Department of Consumer Affairs:

- Rule 27 registration for packaged-commodity manufacturers, packers and importers.
- Section 19 importer registration for weights and measures.
- Section 22 model approval for weights and measures or weighing/measuring instruments.

## Official evidence

- [Legal Metrology official portal](https://lm.doca.gov.in/index.aspx): identifies the three routes and directs relevant applications to the official portal or NSWS.
- [Department of Consumer Affairs Legal Metrology Act and rules](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act): provides the Act, Packaged Commodities Rules, Approval of Models Rules and continuing amendments.
- [Weights and measures overview](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/weight-and-measures): records importer registration, model approval and State enforcement-rule boundaries.
- [Rule 27 packaged-commodities portal](https://lm.doca.gov.in/pcr/): official application route.
- [Importer registration route](https://lm.doca.gov.in/oregis/): official Section 19 route.
- [Packaged-commodities certificate list](https://lm.doca.gov.in/pcr/certificates): current operational evidence that Rule 27 registrations are being issued.

## Editorial decisions

- The three routes are separate because one registration or approval does not automatically satisfy the others.
- No financial benefit or amount is recorded.
- State/UT enforcement, inspection, verification, fees and product-specific approvals remain explicit unknowns.
- All records are `in_review`, use unreviewed structured rules and cannot publish automatically.
