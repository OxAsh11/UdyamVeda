## Reconciliation recorded

- The current portal describes 25% subsidy for eligible SC/ST MSEs purchasing new plant, machinery or equipment through institutional credit, with an overall subsidy ceiling of INR 25 lakh.
- The guideline describes institutional finance up to INR 1 crore for the relevant asset route, with subsidy calculated at 25% subject to the INR 25 lakh subsidy cap. Loan, asset cost and subsidy are separate values.
- Applications are lender-led through the concerned Prime Lending Institution; nodal banks/agencies upload claims. The portal is not a direct UdyamVeda filing route.
- The January 2026 checklist provides operational document evidence but does not, by itself, extend the NSSH financial cycle or establish a universal new-intake window.
- The cited 2025 revised guideline covers the 2021-22 to 2025-26 cycle ending 31 March 2026. Dashboard reporting, including 2026-27 context, is not treated as a successor legal cycle.
- Trading activity is excluded; manufacturing/service, SC/ST ownership, new-asset, PLI and applicable pollution-clearance conditions must be verified.
