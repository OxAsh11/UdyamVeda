# GREAT source audit — 2026-09-09

## Decision

Create a paused, review-gated structured draft for the GREAT technical-textiles startup grant. Do not publish it as an open application opportunity.

## Authority and evidence

- The Ministry of Textiles is the IGOD authority; the National Technical Textiles Mission is the programme route.
- The PIB announcement dated 29 August 2023 establishes the launch scope, grant ceiling, duration, incubator allocation and minimum incubatee contribution.
- The Ministry's Rajya Sabha answer dated 13 February 2026 confirms ongoing programme activity, 24 approved startup projects, monitoring and extracted eligibility criteria.
- Startup India Playbook is discovery-only cross-check evidence.

## Fine-print findings

- Maximum support is INR 50 lakh for up to 18 months; it is not a guaranteed entitlement.
- Individual and startup routes are distinct. Individual promoter/shareholders of technical-textiles startups cannot use the individual route.
- Startup thresholds include turnover below INR 100 crore, incorporation age no greater than five years and at least 51% resident-Indian ownership/control.
- The incubator's 10% allocation and the incubatee's minimum 10% contribution are separate from the startup grant ceiling.
- No current open call, deadline, approved incubator list or live submission form was verified in the reviewed official sources.

## Publication guard

The record remains `paused`, its version remains `in_review`, and a reviewer must verify the controlling GREAT guideline, current call, incubator route, grant-release/milestone rules, NOC/full-time conditions, monitoring, IP and current application route before publication.
