## Reconciliation recorded

### Export Promotion Mission

- The mission is approved for FY 2025–26 to FY 2030–31 with a total outlay of ₹25,060 crore.
- Interest subvention for pre/post-shipment export credit and collateral support for export credit are operationalised for MSME exporters.
- Other interventions are being rolled out in phases; the mission outlay is not an individual applicant entitlement.

### Market Access Initiative

- The current Department of Commerce claims monitor reports MAI claims through August 2026.
- MAI remains an agency-led, project-based route. Current reporting does not establish a universal direct exporter application window or applicant ceiling.

### RoDTEP

- DGFT Notification 74/2025–26 continues RoDTEP for eligible exports from 1 April through 30 September 2026.
- Rates and value caps in force on 31 March 2026 continue during that period.
- Product tariff line, cap, customs documentation and no-double-benefit checks remain mandatory.
