## Reconciliation recorded

- The current portal distinguishes TDB Core Funding from RDIF/SLFM and displays current calls. Batch 088 is scoped to Core Funding only.
- The Core Funding page describes separate loan, equity and exceptional-grant modes. The draft keeps them in separate benefit buckets.
- The current FAQ states a general loan up to 50% at 5% simple annual interest and includes a 70%/INR 5 crore committee wording plus a DPIIT-startup exception for qualifying Companies Act entities. These provisions must be reconciled against the controlling guideline, current form and sanction terms before publication.
- The current FAQ says applications are accepted throughout the year and that evaluation typically takes 3–4 months. It also describes a one-year post-completion moratorium and nine half-yearly instalments, while the Core Funding page uses different instalment wording.
- The FAQ states that TDB does not levy processing, administrative or commitment charges, but separately describes a 1% earnest fee that may be forfeited after a letter of intent. These are distinct conditions.
- The Downloads page exposes current revised guidelines/FAQs and forms, which are required for the reviewer to confirm exact documents, submission route and reporting obligations.
- RDIF/SLFM has materially different terms (including TRL 4 onward and 3%–4% rates) and must not be shown as part of this Core Funding opportunity.
