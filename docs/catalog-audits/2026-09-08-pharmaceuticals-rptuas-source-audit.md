## Current structured interpretation

RPTUAS is modeled as a capital-subsidy route, not as a loan or combined financing value. The current maximum is INR 2 crore. The three preceding-year average-turnover bands are INR 1 crore to below INR 50 crore at 20%, INR 50 crore to below INR 250 crore at 15%, and INR 250 crore to below INR 500 crore at 10%.

## Human review gate

The record is paused and remains in_review. Official material records an application window opened on 11 April 2024 and an outlay expected to be fully sanctioned by March 2026, while current Department pages still publish 2026 modifications. That evidence does not prove that a new application intake is open in September 2026. A reviewer must confirm the live Department/SIDBI portal, current budget/intake, new-application status, current certification sequence and any superseding notification before publication or recommendation.

No applicant should be told that RPTUAS is currently open merely because the official scheme page exists.
