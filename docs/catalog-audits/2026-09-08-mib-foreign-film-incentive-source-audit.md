# Ministry of Information and Broadcasting foreign-film incentive source audit

Audit date: 08 September 2026

## Official evidence inspected

- [India Cine Hub Incentive Calculator](https://indiacinehub.gov.in/incentive-calcuator-page) is the current implementing-agency intake page. It presents support of up to 40% on spend in India, asks about Significant Indian Content and 15% or more Indian cast/crew, and states that an online application requires a non-refundable INR equivalent of USD 225 processing fee.
- [India Cine Hub international-projects guide](https://indiacinehub.gov.in/permission/step-by-step-guide/international-projects) identifies India Cine Hub as the single-window route under NFDC and the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting. It lists registration, application, document upload, fee, location permits and visa steps, and states that permission normally takes no more than three weeks after the requisite documents are submitted, subject to longer consultation cases.
- [Ministry of Information and Broadcasting guideline URL](https://mib.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/Revised%20incentive%20guidelines.pdf) is registered as the governing source. The current web research result describes a base reimbursement up to 30% of Indian qualifying production expenditure, a 5% Indian-manpower bonus, a 5% Significant Indian Content bonus and an enhanced INR 30 crore cap. The PDF requires direct availability and revision verification before publication.

## Record interpretation

This is one Central opportunity for international production companies, foreign producers using an Indian line producer or production-services company, and official audio-visual co-productions. The record models the base reimbursement and conditional bonuses separately so the product does not present 40% as a guaranteed amount. The processing fee is stored separately from the benefit.

The current portal excludes documentaries, audio-visual commercials and music videos from the online calculator route. The exact treatment of every project category, qualifying expenditure, claimant, claim timing and cap must be reconciled against the latest accessible guideline.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and review-gated even though the portal is live. A reviewer must confirm the latest guideline file, PDF revision, INR 30 crore cap, eligible categories, qualifying-expenditure definition, claimant, bonus evidence, current application and incentive-claim forms, and any superseding Ministry notification before publication or recommendation ranking.
