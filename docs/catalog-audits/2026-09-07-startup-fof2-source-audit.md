## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- The scheme has a ₹10,000 crore corpus, but does not invest directly in startups.
- The Government participates in SEBI-registered Category I and Category II AIFs; those AIFs invest in startups.
- The aggregate contribution from Government fund-of-funds programmes to a selected AIF is limited to 50% of that AIF’s corpus, subject to the VCIC’s discretion to adopt a lower limit.
- The operational expense cap is 0.50% per annum.
- Annexure-I records segment-specific AIF life, contribution caps, minimum investment multipliers and the smaller-AIF early-stage/private-placement requirements.
- A startup must be DPIIT-recognized under the latest applicable criteria, but its actual access depends on a selected AIF’s mandate and commercial diligence.
