## Structured interpretation

- The route is intended to bring MSMEs, including non-defence-sector MSMEs in Tier II and Tier III cities, into the defence supply chain through conclaves, workshops, vendor-development meets, seminars, exhibitions and site visits.
- Eligible event-management routes include the Defence Exhibition Organisation, leading think tanks, industry associations, local industry chambers, NSIC and another agency or entity recommended through the Department of Defence Production route.
- National-level event sponsorship is stated up to INR 3 lakh per event; state-level event sponsorship is stated up to INR 1.5 lakh per event.
- The sponsorship amounts belong to approved events/organisers. They are not direct cash entitlements for every participating MSME, and participation does not guarantee procurement, a contract, export business or funding.

## Organizer boundary

The record separates the event-organiser route from the participating-MSME market-access route. A recommendation must first determine which role the business has; an MSME participant is not silently treated as the sponsorship applicant.
