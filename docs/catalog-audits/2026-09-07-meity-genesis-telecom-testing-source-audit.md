# MeitY GENESIS and telecom testing reimbursement source audit

Date: 2026-09-07  
Status: review-gated; not approved for publication

## Scope

This batch adds two distinct technology-business records:

- GENESIS: MeitY ecosystem and startup support focused on Tier-II and Tier-III cities, with component-specific ideation, pilot, investment and deep-tech routes.
- Telecom testing/certification reimbursement: DoT/TEC reimbursement for eligible telecom start-ups and MSEs.

## Official evidence

- [MeitY GENESIS scheme page](https://www.meity.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/gen-next-support-for-innovative-startups-genesis-gN5AjNtQWa): describes the ₹490 crore, five-year umbrella scheme, eligible ecosystem entities and major components.
- [MeitY Startup Hub GENESIS page](https://msh.meity.gov.in/schemes/genesis): describes Entrepreneur in Residence, pilot, investment and deep-tech components and selected implementing agencies.
- [GENESIS brochure](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Brochure_GENESIS.pdf): records component-specific historical support boundaries; these are not treated as a universal applicant ceiling.
- [MeitY telecom reimbursement page](https://www.meity.gov.in/offerings/schemes-and-services/details/guidelines-for-implementation-of-scheme-for-reimbursement-of-testing-and-certification-charges-for-start-ups-and-telecom-mses-cjNwMjMtQWa): states 75%/60%/50% reimbursement rates, ₹50 lakh maximum per entity, ₹25 crore allocation and two-year-or-fund-exhaustion duration boundary.
- [TEC/DoT reimbursement guideline](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/06/837f9d009185a3e2061fb12ba8ef4d17.pdf): records application documents, DPIIT/Udyam evidence, eligible-lab evidence, financial declarations and no-duplicate-reimbursement undertaking.

## Editorial decisions

- GENESIS values are stored as component-specific facts, not combined into one misleading amount.
- GENESIS is treated as an implementing-agency selection route; the scheme budget is not an applicant entitlement.
- Telecom testing reimbursement stores the ₹50 lakh entity cap and rates separately from the ₹25 crore scheme allocation.
- Both records are `in_review`, use unreviewed structured rules and cannot publish automatically.
