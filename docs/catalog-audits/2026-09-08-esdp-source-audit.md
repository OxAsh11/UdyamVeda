# Entrepreneurship and Skill Development Programme source audit — 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers ESDP under the Ministry of MSME Development of MSMEs vertical. It is primarily a training and capacity-building route delivered through approved implementing organisations, not a universal cash grant to founders.

## Official evidence reviewed

- Approved ESDP Guidelines dated 25 March 2022: https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/Approved_ESDP_Guidelines.pdf
- MyMSME portal scheme context: https://my.msme.gov.in/mymsme/Reg/home.aspx
- MSME ATI/module context: https://ati.msme.gov.in/ModuleHome.aspx

## Evidence interpretation

- The guideline defines EAP, E-SDP and MDP tracks, including advanced variants.
- It prioritises entrepreneurship and skill development, support for new and existing MSMEs, and preference groups including SC, ST, women, differently abled, ex-servicemen and BPL participants.
- Implementing agencies propose programmes and publish calendars through the MIS; the applicant-facing availability is therefore programme, institution and location dependent.
- The guideline states a 40% weaker-section target for E-SDP beneficiaries and fee exemptions for specified groups. These are programme conditions, not a guaranteed monetary benefit to each participant.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and publication-blocked until a reviewer confirms the current guideline version, programme calendar, participant documents, age and course-specific qualifications, implementing-organisation route, fee rules, attendance/certification conditions and whether an actual application window is open.
