# Design Linked Incentive source audit

Audit date: 2026-09-08

This draft record separates the Product Design Linked Incentive, Deployment Linked Incentive and non-cash design-infrastructure routes. It remains `in_review` and is not published.

Primary sources:

- [MeitY DLI Gazette notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Notification-Design-Linked-Incentive-Scheme.pdf) — pages 6-8, paragraphs 2-11.
- [DLI official FAQ](https://chips-dli.gov.in/DLI/Faq) — applicant categories, support ceilings, application fee and C-DAC disbursement route.
- [DLI application route](https://chips-dli.gov.in/DLI/applyforDLI) — registration, DPR, fee and fiscal-support procedure.
- [MeitY DLI guidelines](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/Guidelines%20for%20Design%20Linked%20Incentive%20%28DLI%29%20Scheme%20-%2030.12.2022.pdf) — operational application and claim detail.

Confirmed from the notification:

- Eligible applicant categories are domestic companies, startups and MSMEs engaged in eligible semiconductor design.
- Product Design Linked Incentive is up to 50% of eligible expenditure, capped at INR 15 crore per application.
- Deployment Linked Incentive is 4%-6% of net sales turnover over five years, capped at INR 30 crore per application.
- C-DAC is the nodal implementing agency and fiscal support is milestone/verification based.
- Design-infrastructure access is a separate support bucket and must not be converted into a cash entitlement.
