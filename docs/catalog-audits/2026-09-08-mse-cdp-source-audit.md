# MSE-CDP source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the Micro and Small Enterprises Cluster Development Programme (MSE-CDP), a collective/project-level route for cluster studies, soft interventions, common facility centres and industrial infrastructure. It must not be presented as an automatic individual-enterprise grant.

## Official evidence reviewed

- Ministry of MSME Modified Guidelines of MSE-CDP: https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/ModifiedGuidelinesofMSE.pdf
- Official proposal route retained in the seed: https://www.my.msme.gov.in/MyMsme/Reg/COM_ClusterForm.aspx

## Evidence interpretation

- The guideline is administered by the Office of the Development Commissioner (MSME) and supersedes the earlier SICDP and IID guidance.
- Covered tracks include Diagnostic Study Reports, Soft Interventions, Detailed Project Reports, Common Facility Centres and Infrastructure Development.
- The guideline states up to Rs 2.50 lakh for a cluster Diagnostic Study Report, with staged release after approval and report acceptance.
- Soft interventions generally require at least 25 participating units, or 20 in specified special cases; the guideline states a project-cost ceiling of Rs 25 lakh and a Government grant rate of 75%, or 90% for specified categories.
- Common Facility Centre support is stated as 70% of project cost up to a Rs 15 crore project ceiling, or 90% for specified categories, with SPV, beneficiary contribution, land, approval and operating conditions.
- Infrastructure-development support is stated as 60% of project cost up to Rs 10 crore, or 80% for specified categories, with State/UT and project-level conditions.
