Editorial findings:

- Component A supports seven NIPER Centres of Excellence and is not a generic direct startup grant; startup participation requires an approved CoE or collaboration route.
- Component B directly covers industry, startups and MSMEs across six priority areas with separate early-stage and later-stage TRL routes.
- The official portal describes early-stage startup/MSME assistance up to ₹1 crore fully funded, and above ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore at up to 50%; later-stage support is up to 35% capped at ₹100 crore per project, with strategic-priority projects potentially up to 50%.
- Co-funding and verified financial closure are material; the FAQ describes an eight-week post-conditional-selection closure period before the funding agreement/first disbursement.
- The portal currently says the second call is opening soon and early-stage submissions will use a two-step concept-note/detailed-application process. The record therefore remains call-gated and not publicly actionable yet.
