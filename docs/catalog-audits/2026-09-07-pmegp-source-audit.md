## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- PMEGP is a credit-linked margin-money subsidy for eligible new micro-enterprises. The record keeps subsidy and bank finance in separate value buckets.
- The guideline ceiling for subsidy-supported project cost is ₹50 lakh for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for business/service. Working-capital components are capped at 40% for manufacturing and 60% for service/trading within the stated project limits.
- Beneficiary contribution is 10% for the General category and 5% for the Special category. Margin-money rates vary by category and rural/urban location.
- Existing units under PMRY, REGP or other Government schemes, and units that already received Government subsidy under another scheme, are excluded from the new-unit route.
- New PMEGP units must be registered on the Udyam Portal before physical verification and adjustment of margin money in the beneficiary loan account.
- The current portal terms state that applications are submitted online; only one person from a family is eligible for new-enterprise assistance, and projects without capital expenditure are not eligible for financing.
- The current terms distinguish the existing-unit upgradation route: qualifying PMEGP/MUDRA units must satisfy prior-loan repayment and profitability conditions and use mandatory Udyam registration.
- The current portal is operating with 2026-27 statistics; this supports a rolling application route but does not guarantee fund availability, subsidy, bank sanction or processing time.
- Margin Money is subject to eligibility, admissibility and fund availability; an application does not guarantee subsidy or bank sanction.
- The current portal terms state that EDP training is required before loan release unless exempted, physical verification with geo-tagging and OTP validation may be conducted, and Margin Money remains locked for three years subject to specified refund conditions.
- The current terms require a bilingual project-site signboard and caution that implementing agencies have not authorized consultants or middlemen to guarantee approval.
- The second-loan/upgradation route is distinct from new-unit eligibility and must not be mixed into this record.
