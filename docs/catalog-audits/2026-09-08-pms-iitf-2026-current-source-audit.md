## Reconciliation recorded

- IITF 2026 applications are shown as open from 1 September 2026 through 20 September 2026.
- The official notice requires a valid Udyam Registration as of 31 August 2026 and states that late applications are not considered.
- Stall fee or a receipt/invoice is not required at the application stage and is collected only from MSEs allotted stalls under the PMS guidelines.
- The official portal separates MSE event applications from organiser proposals. The organiser proposal extension from October 2026 onward is not an MSE IITF application deadline.
- The event record remains separate from generic PMS and is represented as a fixed-window event so the dated IITF window cannot be incorrectly applied to all PMS components.
