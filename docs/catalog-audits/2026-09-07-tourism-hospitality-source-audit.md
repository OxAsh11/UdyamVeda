# Tourism and hospitality source audit

Date: 2026-09-07

Record: `incredible-india-bed-breakfast-homestay-classification`

Official evidence:

- [NIDHI+ official portal](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/index.html) — current unit-registration route and notice that fresh voluntary construction/pre-construction project-approval applications are discontinued from 16 March 2026.
- [NIDHI+ guidelines index](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/home/page/guidelines) — Ministry of Tourism index for accommodation and tourism-service-provider guidelines.
- [Common National Standards and Guidelines for Incredible India Bed & Breakfast and Homestay Establishments](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/uploads/gallery/1659456723.pdf) — purpose, State/UT applicability caveat, one-to-six rooms/12-bed boundary, inspection, two-year validity, Silver/Gold fees, documents, correction period and appeal.
- [MyNIDHI official route](https://nidhi.tourism.gov.in/mynidhi/) — current unit handoff target.

Editorial findings:

- This is a recognition/classification and official-handoff route, not a grant, subsidy, loan or guaranteed occupancy benefit.
- The cited guideline applies where the State/UT does not have its own approval/classification/accreditation scheme; State/UT and local permissions remain separate dependencies.
- Homestay classification requires the owner/promoter and family to reside at the establishment, with one to six rooms and no more than 12 beds. Bed-and-breakfast classification uses an owner-appointed resident operator and the same capacity boundary.
- The cited guideline lists a two-year classification validity, Silver fee of Rs. 3,000 and Gold fee of Rs. 5,000. The live portal and current regional route must confirm these values before publication.
- The live portal explicitly closes the voluntary construction/pre-construction project-approval route from 16 March 2026. The record does not recommend that discontinued service.

Status: structured, source-backed, `in_review`; not approved for publication.
