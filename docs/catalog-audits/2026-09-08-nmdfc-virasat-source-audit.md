## Reconciliation recorded

- Virasat is presented by NMDFC as part of the Term Loan framework for minority craftpersons and artisans, supporting working capital and fixed capital for equipment, tools, machinery and raw material.
- The current summary uses Credit Line 1 annual family income up to INR 3 lakh and Credit Line 2 up to INR 8 lakh. It states maximum Virasat credit up to INR 10 lakh under both credit lines.
- The current narrative states 5% p.a. under Credit Line 1, 6% p.a. for male artisans under Credit Line 2 and a 1% concession for female artisans under both credit lines.
- The same official page is internally inconsistent: its comparison table shows different female rates than the narrative. This is a material source conflict and remains unresolved; the record must not publish a definitive female rate until a reviewer identifies the controlling current document.
- The current table states a six-month moratorium and five-year repayment period. Current SCA/partner terms and lending policy remain controlling.
- The route is concessional credit, not a guaranteed grant or free toolkit. Marketing/exhibition support must remain separate from credit.
