## Reconciled facts

- C2S is an umbrella MeitY programme spanning academic/R&D, startup/MSME, industry-academia, Grand Challenge/Hackathon/RFP and design-infrastructure routes.
- Startups and MSMEs can participate in notified project categories, but the umbrella page does not establish one universal startup grant, generic ceiling or current open application window.
- The guidelines require proposal-specific technical scope, novelty, TRL, deliverables, EDA/fabrication/testing plan, commercialization or technology-transfer roadmap, roles, contribution and timelines as applicable.
- EDA access, fabrication support, training, project financial support and institutional infrastructure are separate benefit buckets.
- The BLDC call page is currently marked closed, and the RSC call page records a 22 July 2026 deadline. These route-specific states do not prove that every C2S route is permanently closed, nor do they prove a current general call is open.

## Publication blockers

- Verify a current startup/MSME or institution-facing C2S call, route, deadline and applicable proposal category.
- Reconcile the controlling category's benefit ceiling, contribution, milestones, evaluation, fund release, PFMS, IP, utilization and reporting terms.
- Keep historical BLDC terms and institution-led RSC terms separate from the generic C2S record.
- Confirm the current portal submission behavior, support contact and grievance route.

## Decision

Create one paused, review-gated umbrella record linked to the C2S lead. Do not recommend it as actionable until a current applicable call is verified; split materially different future calls into separate opportunity versions or records.
