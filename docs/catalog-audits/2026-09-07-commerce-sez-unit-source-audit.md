## Reconciliation recorded

- An approved SEZ unit may use duty-free import/domestic procurement facilities for authorized operations and the current zero-rated IGST route, subject to the Act, Rules, customs/GST documentation and current notices.
- A unit must achieve positive Net Foreign Exchange cumulatively over five years from commencement of production; detailed formula and eligible supplies remain reviewer-controlled.
- Domestic Tariff Area sales are subject to full customs duty and import-policy conditions.
- Historical Section 10AA income-tax exemption text appears on official legacy pages with a sunset clause for new units from 2020. It is explicitly excluded from the current benefit summary.
- The SEZ Online help states that a proposed unit submits an application to the concerned Development Commissioner; the route is an approval and compliance workflow, not a cash grant.

## Human review gate

- Confirm the specific SEZ, authorized operation, sector restrictions, entity/IEC/PAN requirements, current forms, LOA process, user charges and current Rule amendments.
- Verify the positive-NFE plan and all customs/GST evidence before producing a recommendation.
- Check state/local approvals, land/lease conditions, labour and sector licences separately.
- Do not publish historical income-tax, MAT, CST or service-tax text as a current automatic entitlement, and do not represent SEZ approval as a guaranteed subsidy or tax refund.
