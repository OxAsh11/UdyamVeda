# Technology Development Board source audit

Record: `technology-development-board-technology-commercialisation-finance`

Status: structured, source-backed and `in_review`; not approved for publication.

Editorial findings:

- TDB is directly relevant to Indian technology-led companies, eligible cooperatives and eligible R&D agencies developing or commercialising indigenous technology or adapting imported technology.
- The general funding page describes a soft loan up to 50% of project cost at 5% simple interest, equity up to 25% of project cost and grants only in exceptional cases. These are separate instruments and are not added into one benefit value.
- The FAQ contains a possible committee exception describing up to 70% and up to INR 5 crore. This conflicts with the general funding-mode page and is explicitly preserved as an unresolved review item rather than being treated as a user entitlement.
- The cited guidelines exclude sole entrepreneurs, sole proprietorship concerns and partnership concerns from the commercial-enterprise route; the exact legal form and any applicable R&D-agency route require applicant-specific review.
- The FAQ describes a throughout-the-year submission route, but current online availability, forms, security, royalty, milestones, repayment and sanction conditions require confirmation from the live Board route.
- Approval bands are governance thresholds and must not be shown as applicant benefit ceilings.

The record is intentionally review-gated until a human reviewer reconciles the current guideline, FAQ exception, proposal forms and live submission route.
