## Reconciliation recorded

- The current FTP Chapter 5 states zero-duty treatment for qualifying capital goods, subject to applicable conditions and notifications, with a specific export obligation equal to six times duties, taxes and cess saved, to be fulfilled over six years.
- Manufacturer exporters, qualifying merchant exporters tied to supporting manufacturers, service providers and certain common service providers are covered subject to the current FTP/HBP route.
- The current FTP states that the authorisation is valid for import for 24 months and is not revalidated. The older DGFT FAQ states 18 months; the record preserves this conflict and requires current FTP/HBP and live-portal confirmation before publication.
- Indigenous sourcing, green-technology and specified North-East/Jammu and Kashmir/Ladakh reductions are kept as separate conditional routes, not universal benefits.
- This is not a grant or fixed rupee entitlement. The applicant exchanges duty treatment for export obligations, actual-user/installation conditions, evidence and eventual EODC or regularisation.

## Human review gate

- Confirm current FTP/HBP versions, customs notifications, negative-list exclusions, fees, live attachment list, IEC/RCMC requirements and portal behaviour.
- Check the applicant's specific capital goods, export type, duty-saved calculation, annual-average obligation and special-category claims.
- Review Policy Circular No. 10/2025-26 only for qualifying sector/product-group average-EO relief; do not apply it automatically.
- Do not publish an estimated duty saving, six-times obligation or special reduction as an automatic entitlement without applicant-specific evidence and current DGFT confirmation.
