## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- IEC is generally compulsory for import and export of goods, subject to the exempted categories in the current foreign-trade rules and procedures.
- The official fee schedule identifies an INR 500 fee for an IEC application and describes annual IEC updation during April-June.
- Application identity must be consistent with PAN, bank and entity records. An incomplete or unauthorised application can be rejected and may need a fresh or corrected route.
- IEC does not itself authorize every product or shipment; restricted, prohibited, regulated and product-specific permissions remain separate.
