## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- PMMY provides institutional collateral-free credit for micro-enterprises, including non-agricultural activities allied to agriculture such as poultry, dairy and beekeeping.
- The current categories are Shishu up to ₹50,000, Kishor above ₹50,000 up to ₹5 lakh, Tarun above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh, and Tarun Plus above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh.
- Tarun Plus requires the entrepreneur to have availed and successfully repaid a previous Tarun-category loan, with the current page identifying the route from 24 October 2024.
- Applications are routed through Member Lending Institutions, including banks, NBFCs and MFIs. Collateral is not required under the stated scheme feature, but sanction, pricing, repayment, documents and lender conditions remain separate.
- PMMY is a loan, not a grant, subsidy or guaranteed approval.
