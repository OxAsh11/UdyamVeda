## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- EPFO provides online employer registration and a common EPFO/ESIC registration route. Its Ease of Doing Business guidance states that a bank account and company seal are not required for establishment registration and describes registration as free of cost.
- EPFO employer guidance refers to 20 or more employees for specified establishment types, while the EPFO FAQ recognises voluntary coverage for establishments below that figure. This is not treated as a universal automatic rule.
- ESIC material describes coverage for factories employing 10 or more persons and for specified establishments such as shops, hotels, restaurants, cinemas, road motor transport and newspaper establishments at 10 or more coverable employees in applicable/notified areas, subject to category and state/UT exceptions.
- The ESIC material states a monthly wage ceiling of INR 21,000, or INR 25,000 for persons with disability.
- UdyamVeda must prepare and explain the route, but the employer must use its own credentials, OTPs and statutory declarations.
