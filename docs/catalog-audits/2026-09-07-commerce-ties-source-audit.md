# Department of Commerce TIES source audit — 2026-09-07

## Scope

This batch adds one institutional Central Government record: the Trade Infrastructure for Export Scheme (TIES). It is intentionally not represented as a direct startup, MSME or exporter grant and is not a direct founder benefit.

## Official evidence reviewed

- TIES revised guidelines for FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26: https://www.commerce.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/TIES-revised-guidelines-FY22-to-FY26.pdf
- TIES 20th Empowered Committee minutes dated 23 July 2025: https://www.commerce.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/MoM_20th-EC_23.07.2025.pdf
- Department of Commerce claim monitoring system, reported through August 2026: https://apps.commerce.gov.in/schemes/schemereport

## Evidence interpretation

- The guideline defines export infrastructure such as testing/certification labs, cold chains, trade promotion centres, dry ports, export warehousing, packaging, SEZs and cargo connectivity.
- Eligible implementing agencies include Central/State Government agencies, Export Promotion Councils, Commodity Boards, SEZ Authorities, recognised apex trade bodies, qualifying Government joint ventures and PPP projects. The agency must own and operate the facility.
- The guideline describes grant-in-aid normally limited to the implementing agency equity or 50% of total equity, up to 80% in specified special-area or low-capacity circumstances, with a normal ceiling of ₹20 crore per infrastructure project.
- The July 2025 minutes record ongoing implementation and a remaining-outlay context. The claim monitor shows TIES activity through August 2026. Neither source is treated as proof of a fresh FY 2026-27 application call.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and is marked closed for new-applicant discovery unless an official extension or successor call is found. Before publication, a reviewer must confirm the current status, successor scheme, remaining committed outlay, implementing-agency route, project-cost exclusions, procurement, land, statutory approvals and undertaking/clawback conditions. It must not be recommended directly to ordinary startups or exporters.
