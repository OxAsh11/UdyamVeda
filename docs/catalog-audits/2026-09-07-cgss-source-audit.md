## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- The scheme is a guarantee route, not direct cash paid by the Government to the startup.
- The startup must be DPIIT-recognized, not in default, not classified as an NPA under applicable RBI guidance, and certified by the Member Institution for guarantee cover.
- The maximum debt eligible for guarantee cover is ₹20 crore per borrower.
- Transaction-based cover is 85% of the amount in default up to ₹10 crore and 75% above ₹10 crore, subject to the per-borrower ceiling.
- Umbrella-based cover is actual losses or 5% of pooled investment, whichever is lower, subject to the ceiling.
- The current Startup India page specifies that eligible NBFCs must be RBI-registered, rated BBB or above by an RBI-accredited external rating agency, and have minimum net worth of ₹100 crore.
- The startup approaches an eligible Member Institution; NCGTC provides the trustee route. UdyamVeda must not represent itself as the lender, trustee or government filing portal.
