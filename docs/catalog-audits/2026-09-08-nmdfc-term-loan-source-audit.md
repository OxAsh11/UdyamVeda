## Reconciliation recorded

- The Term Loan Scheme is for individual beneficiaries from notified minority communities and economically viable, technically feasible income-generating ventures. Applications are routed through SCAs and banking partners.
- The current summary describes Credit Line 1 up to INR 20 lakh and Credit Line 2 up to INR 30 lakh, with income lines of INR 3 lakh and INR 8 lakh and rates of 6%, 8% for male Credit Line 2 beneficiaries and 6% for women Credit Line 2 beneficiaries.
- The Credit Line detail pages describe up to 90% of project cost subject to an NMDFC share cap of INR 18 lakh / INR 27 lakh and at least 5% beneficiary contribution. These are financing-share limits, not a separate extra benefit.
- The FAQ records six-month moratorium, five-year beneficiary repayment, eight-year SCA repayment, three-month utilization and 90:5:5 NMDFC:SCA:beneficiary financing. Current SCA and policy terms remain controlling.
- Headline credit ceilings must not be displayed as guaranteed sanction, cash entitlement or an amount that bypasses project viability, contribution and channel-partner appraisal.
