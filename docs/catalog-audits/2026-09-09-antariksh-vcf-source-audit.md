# Antariksh Venture Capital Fund source audit

Date: 9 September 2026

## Finding

The Startup India June 2026 Playbook lists “Antariksh Venture Capital Fund / Space VCF” under IN-SPACe and the Department of Space. The Playbook describes an equity-oriented SEBI-registered AIF for unlisted Indian space companies, a TRL 4+ criterion, and the SIDBI Venture Capital application route.

The primary operational source is the official SIDBI Venture Capital Limited fund-manager page, not BIRAC. It identifies AVCF as a Category II AIF, names SIDBI Venture Capital Limited as fund manager/sponsor, identifies IN-SPACe as the key investor, states the Indian space-company and TRL 4+ investment criterion, describes privately negotiated equity/convertible/permitted instruments, and provides the contact/application route.

The Department of Space/PIB releases provide supporting institutional and change-detection evidence: the April 2026 release reports SEBI registration, an initial close with INR 1,005 crore committed corpus, and SIDBI Venture Capital Limited as investment manager; the August 2026 reply states the fund is in operation and that its Investment Committee has selected three startups. Neither source establishes an individual company entitlement, universal ticket size, grant, subsidy, loan, or fixed deadline.

## Catalog treatment

- Structured record: `antariksh-venture-capital-fund`
- Status: `paused` / `in_review`
- Benefit: negotiated equity or permitted fund instrument investment
- Not a grant, subsidy, loan, or guaranteed government payment
- Fund corpus is kept separate from applicant-level benefit
- Eligibility rule requires verified Indian company, space-sector activity, unlisted status, TRL 4+, and a current AVCF route
- Current availability, diligence, fund documents, ticket size, valuation, governance, dilution, and exit terms remain human-review items
- The Playbook lead is resolved, but publication remains blocked pending human source, eligibility, window/fine-print, amendment, and factual review
