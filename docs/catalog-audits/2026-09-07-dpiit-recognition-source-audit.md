## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- Eligible forms listed on the current recognition page are Private Limited Company, registered Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership and Cooperative Society.
- The page states an ordinary startup age limit of up to 10 years and an ordinary turnover ceiling below INR 200 crore in any previous financial year; it separately states up to 20 years and INR 300 crore for DeepTech startups.
- The application route is NSWS: create an Investor Account, choose Add Approvals > Central Approvals, find Registration as a Startup, add it to the dashboard, complete the form and submit.
- The page states that the application is filed by the startup using its own details, mobile number and email, on a self-certification basis, and that the Ministry does not charge a fee for the recognition or eligibility certificate.
- DPIIT recognition is not a cash grant. 80-IAC and other tax exemptions are separate post-recognition routes with their own eligibility and approval requirements.
- DeepTech status must not be inferred from an ordinary innovation claim; the current framework and evidence requirements need a separate eligibility review.

## Remaining publication blockers

- Confirm the current form document list, supported file formats and size limits through the live NSWS route.
- Confirm the current recognition status/appeal path and any amendments to the notification.

- Have a reviewer approve the structured rules and the separation between recognition, 80-IAC and other tax routes.
