## Reconciled facts

- The official scheme document describes a seed-capital route for eligible startups and micro/small enterprises in the commercial space sector.
- The earlier official document states an assistance ceiling of up to INR 1 crore, milestone-based release in three or more instalments, a possible first instalment of up to 40 percent, three-year utilization, audit/utilization obligations and startup-owned IPR.
- The same document includes an 80 percent Indian-shareholding condition, promoter/angel investment matching the requested grant, and a limit on prior monetary support from Central or State Government schemes, subject to stated exclusions.
- The current IN-SPACe portal exposes Seed Fund results/use-case information and announcements. That evidence does not prove that a new 2026 application window is open.

## Publication blockers

- Verify the current announcement, closing date and operative application route.
- Reconcile the latest startup/MSE definition, ownership, prior-support, matching-investment and space-sector conditions.
- Confirm eligible costs, milestones, documents, committee process, agreement, audit, utilization and amendment history.
- Keep the INR 1 crore ceiling separate from the actual committee-approved grant and do not present it as guaranteed funding.

## Decision

Create one paused, review-gated record linked to the Playbook lead; do not recommend it as actionable until a current call is verified.
