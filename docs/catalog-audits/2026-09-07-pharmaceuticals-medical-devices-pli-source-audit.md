# Pharmaceuticals Medical Devices PLI source audit

Record: `production-linked-incentive-medical-devices`

Status: structured, source-backed and `in_review`; not approved for publication.

Official sources:

- [Revised Medical Devices PLI guidelines, 25 February 2023](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20Guidelines%20of%20PLI%20-%20MD%20dated%2025.02.2023.pdf) - eligibility, four target segments, project and claim conditions.
- [Medical Devices PLI revised guidelines, 18 August 2022](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20Guidelines%20of%20PLI%20Medical%20Devices%20dated%2018.08.2022_0_0.pdf) - implementation-history comparison.
- [Department of Pharmaceuticals Annual Report 2023-24](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/English%20version%20of%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf) - scheme period, incentive period, 5% rate, applicant caps, approved applicants and released claims.

Editorial findings:

- This is separate from both Pharmaceuticals PLI and Bulk Drugs PLI: it covers selected medical-device manufacturers, four target segments and a greenfield-only route.
- The official implementation report describes 5% of incremental sales for FY 2022-23 through FY 2026-27, with Category A and B applicant caps of INR 121 crore and INR 40 crore respectively.
- The reported framework period is FY 2020-21 through FY 2027-28, with a stated minimum investment threshold of INR 180 crore over three years and a total outlay of INR 3,420 crore.
- Official reporting already records approved applicants and released incentive, so the record is marked `closed_or_claim_only` until current Department/PMA status confirms whether any new-applicant route exists.
- The record remains review-gated until device classification, product segment, greenfield status, incremental-sales calculation, PMA route, claim timing, regulatory evidence, bank guarantee and recovery conditions are manually confirmed.
