Editorial findings:

- The three SbDS components are separate records because they have different applicants, benefits, approval routes and evidence requirements; they must not be collapsed into one generic shipbuilding subsidy.
- The scheme boundary is represented as applications approved from 24 September 2025 through 31 March 2036, subject to the component guidelines and current approval route.
- Greenfield is modeled as cluster-level common-infrastructure capital assistance through the eligible SPV or Central-entity route. The current DGMA overview describes 100% central capital assistance for common maritime infrastructure, but eligible items and sanction controls remain review-critical.
- Brownfield is modeled as existing-shipyard capacity expansion with 25% capital assistance, a current implementation-overview cap of INR 1,500 crore per shipyard, and 15/30/35/20 tranche sequencing. Financial closure and approved-cost evidence are material conditions.
- Risk coverage is modeled as insurance or credit-risk mitigation, not as a direct grant, loan or unconditional Government guarantee. The live nominated agency, product, premium, deductible, exclusions and claim route must be confirmed before publication.

All records remain review-gated until the live intake route, applicable component conditions, current agency/product availability and source anchors are manually confirmed.
