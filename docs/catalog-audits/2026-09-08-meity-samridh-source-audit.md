## Confirmed for review

The current MSH page describes SAMRIDH as an accelerator programme for IT-based startups and displays the result of Cohort 2. It states a target of about 300 tech startups over three years and cohort sizes of 5-10 startups.

The scheme document describes selected accelerators running six-month programmes and providing customer, investor, internationalisation, product and mentoring connections. It describes government investment of up to INR 40 lakh per startup, with equal matching investment by the accelerator or investor up to INR 40 lakh, based on valuation and growth stage.

The scheme document describes equity-linked investment vehicles including SAFE/promissory-note mechanics and allows the appropriate equity, debt or other financing mix. Therefore this record must not describe SAMRIDH as a grant or guaranteed cash subsidy, and the government investment must remain separate from private matching capital.

The second-cohort memorandum concerns enabling-partner selection. Partner selection, startup selection and startup investment are different stages. The current page is not evidence of an open startup application window.

## Open publication blockers

- Current participating/enabling accelerator list and startup application links.
- Current Cohort 2 or later cohort startup window and deadline.
- Current startup eligibility, incorporation, product, stage, ownership and prior-investment rules.
- Current investment instrument, valuation, dilution, rights, conversion, milestones, disbursement, reporting and exit terms.
- Accelerator fees, equity limits, service obligations, matching-investment evidence and grievance route.
- Exact current guideline revision/amendment and human reviewer approval.

## Human review gate

Remain `in_review`. Do not publish a direct-application call, grant amount, guaranteed investment, eligibility decision or combined benefit value until the open blockers are resolved from current official documents and an authorized reviewer approves all required checks.
