## Reconciliation recorded

- The current Term Loan page describes viable industrial, agricultural and service ventures up to unit cost INR 50 lakh, with NSTFDC assistance up to 90% of unit cost through State Channelizing Agencies.
- Promoter contribution is shown as 0% up to INR 1 lakh, 2% above INR 1 lakh to INR 2.50 lakh, 3% above INR 2.50 lakh to INR 5 lakh, and 5% above INR 5 lakh.
- Beneficiary interest is shown as 6% up to INR 5 lakh loan amount, 8% up to INR 10 lakh, and 10% above INR 10 lakh. SCA rates are shown separately as 3%, 5% and 7%.
- Repayment is quarterly within a maximum of five years including the moratorium period; the current page does not state the moratorium duration.
- The eligibility page states ST status for applicants/members, an annual family-income reference of INR 3 lakh, an 80% ST-membership rule for cooperatives and a notified-State residence condition.
- The annual report is supporting historical/reference evidence. Any conflicting income or rate interpretation remains review-gated; the income-reference reconciliation is not yet approved.
