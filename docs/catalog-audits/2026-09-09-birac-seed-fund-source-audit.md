# BIRAC SEED Fund source audit — 2026-09-09

## Decision

Create a paused, review-gated structured draft for BIRAC's Sustainable Entrepreneurship and Enterprise Development (SEED) Fund. Do not publish it as a universally open startup opportunity.

## Authority and evidence

- BIRAC is the implementing agency under the Department of Biotechnology.
- The official BIRAC guideline establishes the incubator framework, Indian-ownership threshold, startup investment ceiling, equity/equity-linked instruments, governance, reporting and exit provisions.
- The current BIRAC page lists partner BioNEST incubators and a startup-facing partner route, but its displayed impact figures are marked February 2023.
- The Startup India Playbook and ISTI portal are discovery and cross-check sources; BIRAC controls the operational record.

## Fine-print findings

- The startup benefit is up to INR 30 lakh in equity or equity-linked instruments, not a non-repayable grant.
- The incubator-level INR 100-200 lakh grant-in-aid and startup-level INR 30 lakh investment ceiling are separate value buckets.
- The startup must be a Companies Act entity with at least 51% shareholding by Indian citizens; OCI/PIO holdings do not satisfy the guideline's mandatory condition.
- The incubator selects startups, executes the investment agreement, monitors milestones and controls partner-level intake, valuation, dilution and instrument terms.
- No universal current startup call, deadline or application form was verified.

## Publication guard

The record remains `paused`, its version remains `in_review`, and a reviewer must verify the current partner list, partner-level intake, funding availability, instrument/valuation/dilution terms, diligence, IP, governance, amendments and current application route before publication.
