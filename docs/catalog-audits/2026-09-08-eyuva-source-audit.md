# E-YUVA source audit — 2026-09-08

## Decision

E-YUVA is resolved from the Startup India Playbook into a structured, paused and review-gated record. It supports students and researchers through category-specific fellowship/research support, E-YUVA Centre incubation and mentoring. It is not a general incorporated-company, MSME or startup grant.

## First-party sources reviewed

- [BIRAC E-YUVA current page](https://birac.nic.in/e_yuva.php): categories, E-YUVA Centres, domains and `NO ACTIVE CALLS` state.
- [E-YUVA scheme guidelines](https://birac.nic.in/webcontent/1643177664_EYUVA_Guidelines.pdf): EYC and fellow eligibility, separate benefits, durations, selection, IP and reporting terms.
- [BIRAC 3rd E-YUVA Fellow call](https://www.birac.nic.in/cfp_view.php?id=112&scheme_type=41): historical undergraduate team route, 12 participating centres and 15 October–15 December 2025 window.
- [BIRAC proposal submission guide](https://www.birac.nic.in/howtoSubmitProposal.php): official account and online submission route.

## Structured facts retained

- Innovation Fellows: post-graduate and post-doctoral category with separate monthly fellowship and annual research-grant amounts, an 18-month term and limited extension conditions.
- E-YUVA Fellows: undergraduate teams of up to five, mentor/E-Cell or institutional support, 12-month fellowship, quarterly stipend and team research grant.
- E-YUVA Centres provide pre-incubation space, equipment, workshops and BioNEST-linked mentoring; these are separate non-cash/institution-mediated benefits.
- The current BIRAC page states `NO ACTIVE CALLS`; the reviewed 2025 undergraduate call is historical and cannot create a current deadline.

## Publication gate

The record remains `paused`, `in_review`, and `publicationAllowed: false` until a reviewer verifies a current call, component, centres, dates, citizenship and degree conditions, mentor/E-Cell requirements, current amounts, milestones, reporting, IP, agreement and centre-specific instructions.
