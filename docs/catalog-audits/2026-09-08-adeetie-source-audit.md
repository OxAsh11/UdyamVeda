# ADEETIE source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the Assistance in Deploying Energy Efficient Technologies in Industries & Establishments (ADEETIE) scheme. It must be represented as conditional interest subvention and technical support, not as a grant, a guaranteed loan or an unconditional subsidy.

## Official evidence reviewed

- BEE ADEETIE current eligibility page: https://www.beeindia.gov.in/show_content.php?lang=1&level=1&lid=384&ls_id=234
- Official ADEETIE portal linked by BEE: https://adeetie.beeindia.gov.in/

## Evidence interpretation

- ADEETIE is a Ministry of Power initiative implemented by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency.
- The BEE page states coverage across 60 industrial clusters and 14 energy-intensive sectors.
- It states 5% interest subvention for Micro and Small enterprises and 3% for Medium enterprises, with a minimum net borrowing interest rate of 2%.
- Investment Grade Energy Audit and Detailed Project Report reimbursement is stated as actual cost up to Rs 75,000 for Micro/Small enterprises and Rs 1,00,000 for Medium enterprises per project or loan.
- Eligibility requires Udyam registration, an identified covered cluster and sector, and an eligible bank or financial-institution loan between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 15 crore.
- The BEE page states an implementation period of FY 2025-26 through FY 2027-28.
