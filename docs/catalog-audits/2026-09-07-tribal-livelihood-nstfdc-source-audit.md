# Ministry of Tribal Affairs, PMJVM, VCF-ST and NSTFDC source audit

Date: 2026-09-07

This batch adds four review-gated Central records:

- PMJVM tribal value-chain support
- Venture Capital Fund for Scheduled Tribes (VCF-ST)
- NSTFDC Term Loan for Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs
- NSTFDC assistance for TRIFED-empanelled tribal artisans

The evidence set uses the official [Ministry of Tribal Affairs livelihood page](https://tribal.gov.in/Livelihood.aspx), the [FY 2026-27 financial summary](https://tribal.gov.in/PFMS_SentionDtSchemewise2627.aspx), [TRIFED Van Dhan](https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/index.php/en/pmvdy), [TRIFED artisan empanelment](https://trifed.tribal.gov.in/en/node/950), [NSTFDC](https://nstfdc.tribal.gov.in/) and the [NSTFDC 2024-25 annual report](https://nstfdc.tribal.gov.in/CuteSoft_Client/writereaddata/upload/NSTFDC_AR%20-%202024-25_Final_03-12-25_English.pdf).

## Editorial boundaries

- PMJVM is represented as project/value-chain support, not a universal individual grant. Its public livelihood page describes a 2021-22 to 2025-26 vision while the FY 2026-27 financial page shows a current allocation line; continuation, component rules and State routes require review.
- VCF-ST is represented as potential venture finance, not a grant, fixed ticket or guaranteed investment. The Ministry overview names IFCI VCF Ltd as manager but does not establish a current open founder call, instrument or ticket size.
- NSTFDC project cost, finance share, beneficiary rates and income references are stored as separate fields. They are not combined into a guaranteed sanctioned amount.
- TRIFED empanelment/market access and NSTFDC credit are separate routes. Individual and SHG/cooperative artisan ceilings remain separate.

## Human review gate

All four records remain `in_review`, with unreviewed structured rules and open publication tasks. Before any record becomes public, a reviewer must reconcile the current guideline/call, applicant route, ST or group evidence, current ceilings, contribution, rates, security, repayment, State availability, documents and amendment history.
