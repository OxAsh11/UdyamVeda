# PACE source audit — 2026-09-08

## Decision

PACE is resolved from the Startup India Playbook into a structured, paused and review-gated record. It is an academia-led translational research programme with separate AIR and CRS components; it is not a general startup or MSME grant.

## First-party sources reviewed

- [BIRAC PACE current page](https://birac.nic.in/desc_new.php?id=286): AIR/CRS scope, eligibility, funding boundaries, online route and recurring call timing.
- [Revised PACE guidelines](https://www.birac.nic.in/webcontent/1729751841_Revised_PACE_guidelines_oct.pdf): proposal, evaluation, funding, IP, monitoring, termination and component terms.
- [BIRAC PACE/i4 2025 RFP](https://birac.nic.in/webcontent/1745322583_i4_rfp_22_04_2025.pdf): historical call scope, TRL boundaries and deadline; not current intake evidence.
- [BIRAC proposal submission guide](https://www.birac.nic.in/howtoSubmitProposal.php): institution registration and PACE proforma route.

## Structured facts retained

- AIR supports academic proof-of-concept/prototype development, with a reviewed total project-cost ceiling of INR 50 lakh and a 10% non-recurring-cost cap.
- CRS validates an academic PoC through an industrial partner/LLP; the reviewed guideline states no funding ceiling, which is not treated as a guaranteed entitlement.
- Academia is the primary route. Industry is optional for AIR and mandatory for CRS, with component-specific Indian ownership, facility, IP and first-right conditions.
- BIRAC describes two regular windows, 1 April–15 May and 1 September–15 October, but a live RFP is still required before showing an actionable deadline.

## Publication gate

The record remains `paused`, `in_review`, and `publicationAllowed: false` until a reviewer verifies the active AIR/CRS call, accreditation, PI evidence, proof-of-principle/PoC, TRL, collaborator and IP terms, budget, milestones, monitoring and amendments.
