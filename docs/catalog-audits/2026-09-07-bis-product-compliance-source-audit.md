# BIS product certification and registration source audit — 2026-09-07

This batch adds three Central, review-gated routes to the catalog:

- BIS Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS)
- BIS registration of jewellers for hallmarked gold and silver articles
- BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) for notified Electronics and IT products

## Editorial boundaries

- These are regulatory certification or registration routes, not grants, loans, subsidies or savings estimates.
- FMCS and CRS remain separate: the current product notification and Indian Standard must determine the route.
- The jeweller record does not imply that registration itself hallmarks or approves articles.
- Product lists, QCOs, mandatory districts, exemptions, fees, testing, validity and surveillance require human verification before publication.
- The records are `in_review`; no record is publication-allowed until an authorised reviewer confirms every source anchor and current condition.

## Human review gate

Before publishing, verify the live BIS portal, product or article scope, current notification, applicable Indian Standard, applicant/representative conditions, test evidence, fees, validity, marking, surveillance, exemptions and amendment history. UdyamVeda prepares and explains the route; it does not impersonate BIS or submit a government application as the user.
