# Performance and Credit Rating Scheme source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the Performance and Credit Rating Scheme for Micro and Small Enterprises. It must be represented as rating and reimbursement support, not as a loan, credit guarantee or approval of financing.

## Official evidence reviewed

- MyMSME Performance and Credit Rating scheme page: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsmeMob/MsmeScheme/Pages/1_2_0.html
- MyMSME scheme index: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsme/Scheme.aspx
- MyMSME scheme proposal context: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsme/SchemeWiseProposals.aspx
- Ministry of MSME guideline route listed by the official scheme index: https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/PCRSchemeGuidelines.pdf

## Evidence interpretation

- The scheme provides an independent third-party performance and credit-worthiness assessment through empanelled rating agencies, covering operational, financial, business and management risk.
- The official MyMSME page states that an enterprise registered in India as a micro or small enterprise may apply and may choose an empanelled rating agency.
- The page lists turnover-based partial fee reimbursement: 75% or a ceiling of Rs 25,000 for turnover up to Rs 50 lakh; 75% or Rs 30,000 for turnover above Rs 50 lakh up to Rs 200 lakh; and 75% or Rs 40,000 above Rs 200 lakh, whichever is less.
- The NSIC route and current agency list, application form, payment sequence, turnover definition and reimbursement availability require current reviewer confirmation.
- MyMSME proposal counts are operational context and do not prove current eligibility, approval likelihood or available budget.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and publication-blocked until a reviewer reconciles the detailed guideline, live NSIC route, empanelled agencies, current reimbursement terms, required documents, application process and any superseding notice.
