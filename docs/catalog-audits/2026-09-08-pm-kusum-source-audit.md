# PM-KUSUM source audit

Audit date: 08 September 2026

## Official evidence inspected

- [MNRE PM-KUSUM scheme page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/pradhan-mantri-kisan-urja-suraksha-evam-utthaan-mahabhiyaan-pm-kusum/) is the current official Ministry source. It states the three components, objective, beneficiary categories, assistance structure and period.
- The page states the scheme period as till 31 March 2026. It describes Component A plants of 500 kW to 2 MW, Component B standalone solar agricultural pumps up to 7.5 HP, and Component C individual-pump and feeder-level solarisation routes.
- The page states 30% central financial assistance for the general Component B and Component C routes against the lower of benchmark or tender cost, 50% for specified special regions, and separate State subsidy and beneficiary-finance conditions.
- The page directs applicants to State/DISCOM/implementing-agency routes and the official PM-KUSUM portal. It also records allocation, sanction, completion-report and utilisation-certificate dependencies.

## Record interpretation

The record is closed at the national level because the current MNRE page states the period ended 31 March 2026. Component-specific assistance remains recorded as historical/current-guideline context, but the record does not claim a post-period extension or a universal live application window. Central assistance, State support, beneficiary finance, market access and institutional feeder-level support remain separate value buckets.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and review-gated. A reviewer must verify any official extension, state allocation, designated implementing agency, live window, vendor route, benchmark/tender-cost treatment and current beneficiary requirements before surfacing a state-specific actionable route.
