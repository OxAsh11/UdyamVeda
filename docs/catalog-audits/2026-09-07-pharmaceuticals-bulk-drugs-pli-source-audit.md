# Pharmaceuticals Bulk Drugs PLI source audit

Record: `production-linked-incentive-bulk-drugs-ksm-di-api`

Status: structured, source-backed and `in_review`; not approved for publication.

Official sources:

- [Bulk Drugs PLI Gazette notification](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Gazettee%20notification%20of%20bulk%20drug%20schemes_0.pdf) - 41 products, objective, rate schedule, greenfield condition, investment thresholds, tenure and application/disbursement framework.
- [Bulk Drugs PLI guidelines](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Guidelines%20for%20the%20Production%20Linked%20Incentive%20%28PLI%29%20Scheme%20for%20promotion%20of_0.pdf) - operating and claim details.
- [12 March 2024 SCOD/bank-guarantee amendment](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Corrigendum%20dated%2012.03.2024.pdf) - revised SCOD, bank-guarantee, multiple-product and non-guarantee-of-disbursement conditions.
- [25 September 2024 successor-in-interest amendment](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Corrigendum%20Under%20PLI%20scheme%20for%20Bulk%20Drugs%2025.09.2024_0.pdf) - successor-in-interest definition.
- [Department application notice](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Notice%20PLI%20Scheme%20for%20Bulk%20Drug.pdf) - historic product-specific Vitamin B1 call and route; not evidence of a current open call.

Editorial findings:

- This is a separate opportunity from the finished/high-value Pharmaceuticals PLI: it targets 41 identified KSM, DI and API products and requires a greenfield project.
- The Gazette records target-segment investment thresholds of INR 400 crore, INR 50 crore, INR 50 crore and INR 20 crore for the four segment groups, with distinct fermentation and chemical-synthesis incentive schedules.
- The framework states a 120-day application window and scheme tenure through FY 2029-30, but the application notices are historic and product-specific. The record therefore preserves `closed_or_claim_only` uncertainty rather than presenting a new application as available.
- The 2024 amendments are material: SCOD can be revised by the competent authority, bank guarantees can be invoked for failure, separate product approvals/guarantees can apply, and approval is not a guarantee of incentive disbursement.
- The record remains review-gated until current selected-applicant/claim status, the controlling product list, implementing route, product-level eligibility, production/sales evidence and recovery conditions are manually confirmed.
