# Medical Device Parks source audit

Record: `promotion-of-medical-device-parks`

Status: structured, source-backed and `in_review`; not approved for publication.

Official sources:

- [Medical Device Parks guidelines](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/Guidelines%20of%20the%20Scheme%20Promotion%20of%20Medical%20Devices%20Parks_1.pdf) - State proposer, common-infrastructure scope, four-park design, grant limits and implementation route.
- [Department of Pharmaceuticals Annual Report 2023-24](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/English%20version%20of%20Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf) - scheme period, selected States, project status and grant details.
- [Department of Pharmaceuticals Annual Report 2020-21](https://pharmaceuticals.gov.in/sites/default/files/english%20Annual%20Report%202020-21.pdf) - application-window closure and initial scheme boundary.

Editorial findings:

- This is cluster infrastructure support, not manufacturer PLI and not a direct grant to each unit in a park.
- The framework provides one-time assistance for common infrastructure: generally 70% of project cost, 90% for North Eastern and specified hilly States, capped at INR 100 crore per park; total outlay is INR 400 crore for four parks.
- The official reports identify Himachal Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and Uttar Pradesh as selected States and place the scheme period at FY 2020-21 through FY 2024-25.
- The application window closed on 15 October 2020. The record is therefore `closed` for new State proposals; current park operation, access, user charges and any successor route require live confirmation.
- The record remains review-gated until each selected park’s current common-facility availability, State/SIA contact, unit-access requirements and successor/extension status are manually confirmed.
