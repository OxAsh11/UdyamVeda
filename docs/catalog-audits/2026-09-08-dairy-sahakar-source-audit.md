## Reconciliation recorded

- Dairy Sahakar is cooperative project finance, not a universal grant or automatic subsidy.
- The official material covers dairy infrastructure, procurement, processing, quality, value addition, packaging, branding, transport, storage, exports and supporting activities such as feed, equipment, ICT, renewable energy, veterinary services, education and capacity development.
- The compendium records no fixed minimum or maximum financial-assistance limit for eligible cooperative projects, but this remains subject to viability, assessment, security, current NCDC terms and permissions. It is not a guaranteed applicant amount.
- The compendium records a 5-8 year loan period with a 1-3 year principal moratorium. Direct infrastructure funding is up to 70% with minimum 30% society share; the NCDC-to-State route is up to 90% with minimum 10% society share at that level. Margin money and working capital are separate, assessment-dependent buckets.
- The launch notice's Rs 5,000 crore figure is a scheme-level 2021 earmark and must not be surfaced as a user-specific benefit, current corpus balance or approval.
