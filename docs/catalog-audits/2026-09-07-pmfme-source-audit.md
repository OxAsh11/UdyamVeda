# PMFME source audit — 7 September 2026

Status: `in_review`; current notices exist, but the applicable component and State/UT route require review.

## Official sources checked

- MoFPI portal: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/
- MoFPI notices and guidelines index: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/pmfme/newsletters/
- Individual/group modification guideline: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/massets/documents/advertisement-doc/PMFME_Guidelines_Modification.pdf

## Reconciled facts

- The MoFPI portal describes PMFME as an all-India Centrally Sponsored scheme implemented with State and UT Governments.
- The portal currently describes component-specific support, including 35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to Rs 10 lakh for eligible individual units, common infrastructure support, SHG seed capital, branding/marketing support and capacity building.
- The portal displays a 30 December 2025 clarification concerning primary-processing applications and a modification notice for individual and group components.
- The portal exposes State/UT, district, ODOP, training and application resources. These are not interchangeable with a single national application window.

## Publication blockers

- Reconcile the current notices and modifications with the base guideline before finalizing individual-unit rules.
- Confirm whether a current State or district intake is available before showing a deadline or actionable application state.
- Keep individual, group, common-infrastructure, SHG, branding and training benefits separate.

## Decision

Keep the existing individual-unit dossier review-gated. Do not silently infer current State-level availability from the national portal or historical guidance.
