## Reconciliation recorded

- WEP is a discovery, matching and ecosystem platform. It is not a direct grant, loan, investment, certification or filing entitlement.
- WEP's access-to-finance page mixes Government schemes, financial-institution resources, State schemes and partner/knowledge content. The underlying authority must control every canonical scheme record.
- The current mentorship route supports women entrepreneurs and women-led collective enterprises, uses profile-based Smart Match, and describes free/pro-bono individual mentoring. A match does not guarantee acceptance or a mentor relationship.
- WEP's disclaimer states that external content may not be complete or accurate and that users should verify details with the respective authorities. WEP therefore remains a discovery/cross-check source in UdyamVeda's source hierarchy.
- Partner content, current counts, mentor availability, events and programme windows are dynamic and must not be published as fixed facts without a dated verification.
