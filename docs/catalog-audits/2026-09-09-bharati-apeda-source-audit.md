# APEDA BHARATI source audit

## Finding

The Startup India Playbook lead BHARATI resolves to APEDA's official BHARATI export-enablement acceleration programme. The official APEDA pages identify a real route for agri-food startups, export-enabling technology/service startups, SPS solution providers, exporters, FPCs and certain individual innovators.

The reviewed pilot application window is closed. BHARATI is not a direct grant or investment scheme: its stated support is structured acceleration, knowledge and market inputs, certification guidance, potential buyer/event exposure, optional paid one-to-one mentorship and best-effort investor outreach. APEDA expressly disclaims guaranteed funding, grants, investment and automatic access to other Government benefits.

## Fine-print boundaries

- Startups generally need an Indian private limited company, registered partnership or LLP, age no more than five years, turnover not exceeding INR 10 crore in any financial year, no split/reconstruction, and innovation or scalable-model evidence.
- High-value agri-food and export-enabling technology applicants need an MVP or commercialized product; the SPS category can accept an idea, research paper, MVP or validated solution under its stated conditions.
- Applicants choose one category. Shortlisted/selected non-APEDA applicants may need APEDA RCMC before acceleration; individual SPS applicants are expressly exempt from the merchant RCMC requirement.
- APEDA's terms state that only the first 1,000 screened applications were considered for the pilot, up to 200 were shortlisted for a business challenge, and up to 100 entered the final cohort.
- The pilot structure references a 120-hour acceleration programme and optional three-session one-to-one mentorship for INR 5,000. Participants bear participation costs and must follow attendance, conduct, data-use and post-programme reporting terms.
