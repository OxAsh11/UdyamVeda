## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- The GSTN checklist covers proof of business constitution, stakeholder/authorised-signatory photos, appointment of authorised signatory, principal and additional places of business, and clarification documents.
- The listed file types are JPG/PDF, with row-specific limits of 100 KB or 1 MB.
- Multiple-document upload is row-specific: the checklist marks some rows as `YES` and others as `NO`; UdyamVeda must not flatten this into a single generic upload rule.
- A document checklist is not an eligibility determination. GST applicability depends on current law and business facts including state, supplies, turnover, e-commerce and sector conditions.
- UdyamVeda may prepare a checklist and validate file properties, but the applicant must complete the official registration and declarations through the GST portal.
