- SCLCSS is represented as a component under NSSH. Because the cited NSSH cycle ended on 31 March 2026 and no successor continuation was evidenced in the sources checked, the national SCLCSS record is also marked `closed`.
- The live portal is preserved as official reference evidence, not treated as proof of a current application window.
