# MAHIR source audit — 2026-09-09

## Decision

Create a review-gated closed structured draft for MAHIR. Do not publish it as an open funding opportunity.

## Authority and evidence

- CPRI is the official programme and call portal for the mission secretariat.
- The Ministry of Power Office Memorandum dated 7 June 2023 is the primary legal/governance source.
- The CPRI first-call PDF is the operational source for the reviewed call and records 20 January 2024 as the submission deadline.
- Startup India Playbook is discovery-only cross-check evidence.

## Fine-print findings

- The mission is planned for 2023-24 to 2027-28, but mission duration does not create a live application window.
- Support is outcome-linked and call/agreement-controlled; there is no verified universal per-startup ceiling.
- Indian startup pilots may be supported and commercialized through the mission.
- Technical and Apex Committee approval applies under the Office Memorandum.
- IP sharing with the Government of India/research agency and non-duplication/non-double-financing declarations must be surfaced before any application handoff.
- The first call is closed. CPRI says another call will be advertised, but a current second call was not verified.

## Publication guard

The record remains `closed`, its version remains `in_review`, and a reviewer must verify any successor call, budget, eligible costs, milestones, selection method, IP agreement, current contact and application route before publication or recommendation.
