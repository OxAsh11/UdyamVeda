## Reconciliation recorded

- The 2024 procurement manual defines a startup as an Indian private limited company, registered partnership or LLP incorporated/registered within 10 years, with turnover not exceeding INR 100 crore in any financial year, pursuing innovation or a scalable business model, and not formed by splitting/reconstructing an existing business; DPIIT recognition is required.
- The manual provides exemption from bid security/earnest money and allows relaxation of prior turnover and prior experience, normally to be ensured subject to quality/technical specifications and stated public-safety, health and critical-security exceptions.
- IREPS is the official procurement platform and its registration guide describes free online registration for new vendors/contractors, with a digital-signing-certificate prerequisite. Tender-specific fees, securities, technical requirements and deadlines still need live verification.
- The catalog benefit is intentionally non-financial. It does not calculate a contract amount, grant amount, procurement value or guaranteed award.
