Status: `closed` opportunity with an `in_review` version; retained for research and prior-claim history, not current publication.

## Reconciliation recorded

- The scheme covered qualifying Indian manufacturers of drones and components, including the listed component categories and software subject to the guidelines.
- Indian MSME/startup annual-sales thresholds were INR 2 crore for drones and INR 0.5 crore for components; Indian non-MSME thresholds were INR 4 crore and INR 1 crore.
- The incentive rate was 20% of eligible value addition in India, with a minimum 40% value-addition threshold. The notification states a total outlay of INR 120 crore and a per-manufacturer cap of INR 30 crore.
- The IFCI portal states that the scheme tenure ended in FY 2023-24 and that the final FY 2023-24 claim deadline was 15 February 2025. The catalog record is therefore closed as of the current audit.
- The 2025 amendment page is retained as evidence requiring extraction; its presence does not by itself prove an extension or successor scheme.
