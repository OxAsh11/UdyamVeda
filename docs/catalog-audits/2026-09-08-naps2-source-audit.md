# NAPS-2 source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the establishment-led National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS-2). It must be represented as capped Central stipend support for eligible apprentices, not as an unrestricted employer grant or a promise of approval.

## Official evidence reviewed

- PIB/MSDE Industry-Aligned Skill Development and Apprenticeship Promotion release, 05 August 2026: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294856&lang=1&reg=1
- National Apprenticeship Portal: https://www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in/
- Apprentices Act, 1961 route referenced by the official release.

## Evidence interpretation

- NAPS continues as NAPS-2 from 2022-23 and operates through participating establishments under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
- The establishment and apprentice route uses the National Apprenticeship Portal, with RDSDE, State Apprenticeship Adviser and eligible Third Party Aggregator support.
- The Government contribution is 25% of the stipend paid by the establishment, capped at Rs 1,500 per apprentice per month during training, and is credited to the apprentice by DBT.
- The employer remains responsible for the prescribed stipend, apprenticeship contract, training, attendance, safety, records and reporting obligations.
- The release describes coverage across designated and optional trades and multiple sectors, including manufacturing, engineering, food processing, IT-ITeS, electronics, healthcare, tourism and services.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and publication-blocked until a reviewer confirms current establishment categories and exclusions, apprentice age/trade/contract rules, stipend schedule, attendance and claim mechanics, Third Party Aggregator route, portal availability and any transition instructions.
