# Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment credit-route source audit

Audit date: 2026-09-07

This batch adds two Central opportunity dossiers, both deliberately kept in `in_review`:

1. Credit Enhancement Guarantee Scheme for Scheduled Castes (CEGSSC)

## Controlled interpretation

- CEGSSC is a guarantee supporting lender facilities; it is not a direct grant, direct loan or guaranteed sanction to the applicant.
- The current IFCI portal states more than 51% SC ownership/shareholding with management control for the past 6 months, guarantee cover from INR 0.15 crore to INR 5 crore, and maximum guarantee tenure of 7 years or the repayment period, whichever is earlier.
- NSFDC loan assistance is for eligible SC beneficiaries with the current FAQ's annual family-income ceiling of INR 5 lakh, subject to amendment.
- NSFDC applications are routed through State Channelizing Agencies or other authorized channel partners; the product tracks must remain separate because ceilings, rates, contribution, moratorium and repayment differ.
- Current FAQ terms, channel-partner availability, lender conditions, forms and amendments require human confirmation before publication.

## Human review gate

Neither record is publishable from this audit alone. A reviewer must reconcile the controlling documents, current implementing portals, product-specific terms, application route, document checklist, amendment history and freshness before approving a version.
