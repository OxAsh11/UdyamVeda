# Coir Vikas Yojana source audit — 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers Coir Vikas Yojana (CVY), a component-based Central Sector scheme administered through the Ministry of MSME and Coir Board. It is not a single universal subsidy; eligibility and benefits depend on the component and participant role.

## Official evidence reviewed

- MSME Coir Vikas Yojana operational guideline: https://msme.gov.in/sites/default/files/Latest%20guidelines-for-CVY.pdf
- MyMSME scheme index: https://my.msme.gov.in/mymsme/Scheme.aspx
- MyMSME component context for Skill Upgradation and Mahila Coir Yojana: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsmeMob/MsmeScheme/Pages/1_3_5_1.html
- MyMSME component context for Development of Production Infrastructure: https://my.msme.gov.in/MyMsmeMob/MsmeScheme/Pages/1_3_5_2.html

## Evidence interpretation

- The guideline covers coir-industry development, domestic and export markets, skills and training, women’s empowerment, entrepreneurship and employment, raw-material utilisation, trade-related services and worker welfare.
- Named components include CITUS, Science and Technology, Skill Upgradation and Mahila Coir Yojana, Export Market Promotion, Domestic Market Promotion, TIRFSS and Welfare Measures.
- The component structure means a coir-sector unit, artisan, trainee, sponsoring institution and implementing participant may have different eligibility and benefit rules.
- The source does not establish a single current application window or a universal amount. Component-specific documents, contribution rules, ceilings, registration conditions and application routes must be preserved separately.
