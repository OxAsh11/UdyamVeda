## Captured boundaries

Eligible existing and upcoming CBG projects must use at least 50% agricultural-residue biomass, have at least 2 TPD installed or proposed capacity, be registered on GOBARdhan, and apply before purchasing machinery. Under-construction projects need at least 50% physical progress under the DPR, and the same machinery cannot receive duplicate Government support.

Assistance is a grant of up to 50% of approved machinery procurement cost or INR 90 lakh per set, whichever is lower. The guideline describes INR 1.8 crore for a 4 TPD project with a pro-rata INR 9 crore project cap. The scheme period is FY 2023-24 to FY 2026-27 and the guideline states 30 September 2026 as the final application date unless extended.

Fine print includes approval before purchase, quarterly intake, DPR appraisal, budget-based selection, invoices and equipment identifiers, physical verification, a five-year bond, insurance for eligible equipment above INR 5 lakh, annual biomass reporting, and possible recovery or benefit suspension for misuse or non-compliance.
