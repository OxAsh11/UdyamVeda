# MSE-ODR source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers the Ministry of MSME's MSE Scheme on Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) for delayed payments. It must be represented as a delayed-payment dispute-resolution and limited assistance route, not as a general grant or automatic recovery guarantee.

## Official evidence reviewed

- Current Ministry of MSME RAMP scheme page: https://ramp.msme.gov.in/ramp/index.php/RAMP-initiative/mse-odr/mse-odr-scheme
- Official MSE-ODR scheme guidelines PDF: https://ramp.msme.gov.in/ramp/sites/default/files/2026-05/odr-guidelines.pdf
- MSME RAMP scheme-guidelines index: https://ramp.msme.gov.in/ramp/scheme-guidelines
- Official ODR portal route linked by the scheme page: https://odr.msme.gov.in/

## Evidence interpretation

- The scheme is a sub-scheme under RAMP and addresses delayed payments faced by Micro and Small Enterprises.
- Eligibility is limited to MSEs registered on Udyam or the Udyam Assist Platform (UAP).
- The guideline states an operational period of 2023-2024 to 2026-2027.
- The scheme component table states a total amount limit of Rs 188.96 crore.
- Financial assistance to an MSE is one-time and capped at Rs 4,000: up to Rs 2,000 or actual cost, whichever is less, for documentation, and up to Rs 2,000 or actual cost, whichever is less, for MSEFC application fees.
- The workflow includes digital guided pathways, e-negotiation, mandatory conciliation and statutory arbitration, with the official ODR portal supporting filing, evidence, hearings, orders, disposal and tracking.
