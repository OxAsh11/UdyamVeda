## Reconciled facts

- The current Open Challenge page shows a 30 September 2026, 11:59 PM deadline and a grant ceiling up to Rs 1.5 crore.
- iDEX identifies startups, MSMEs and individual innovators as applicant categories; the FAQ states that a startup applying to iDEX needs majority Indian stake.
- The programme describes grant-in-aid released against milestones and states that DIO does not take equity. Selection, Product Development Budget approval and the grant agreement remain conditions.
- The broader ADITI ceiling is kept as contextual fine print and is not merged into the Open Challenge benefit.

## Publication blockers

- Verify the live problem statement/focus area, current deadline, extension/closure state and application form.
- Confirm entity, Indian-ownership, proposal, technical, financial and milestone evidence requirements.
- Do not represent grant ceiling as an entitlement or defence procurement guarantee.

## Decision

Keep as a fixed-window review-gated opportunity with a separate Open Challenge benefit bucket.
