## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- CGTMSE is a guarantee mechanism for eligible lender facilities; it is not a direct loan, subsidy or cash grant.
- Current guidance covers eligible credit facilities up to ₹10 crore per borrower. A larger facility may exist, but the guarantee-covered amount remains restricted to the scheme ceiling.
- Coverage is a percentage of the amount in default, not a payment of the percentage to the borrower. Current categories include 85% for micro credit up to ₹5 lakh, 75% for ordinary categories in the stated bands, 90% for women entrepreneurs or MSEs promoted by Agniveers, and 85% for listed SC/ST/PwD/Aspirational District/ZED categories, subject to current rules.
- Hybrid Security permits collateral for part of a facility while the remaining unsecured portion up to the scheme limit may be covered.
- The borrower approaches an eligible Member Lending Institution; the lender appraises, sanctions and lodges the guarantee request.
