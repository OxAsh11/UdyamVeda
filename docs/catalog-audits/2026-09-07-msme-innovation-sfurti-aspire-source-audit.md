## Current SFURTI update — 8 September 2026

- The portal, Ministry dashboard, approved/pending proposal listings and MSME smartboard were checked against the 9 September 2022 revised guideline.
- The homepage snapshot reports 513 approved clusters, 376 functional clusters and 0 proposals under consideration at SSC level. The Ministry dashboard separately reports 137 in-progress clusters, 147 total proposals, 80 at nodal evaluation and 67 at Ministry evaluation. These are source-specific dashboard scopes and must not be combined.
- The guideline states the 100-artisan general / 50-artisan NER-hilly minimum for proposed clusters, proportionate grant consideration for smaller clusters, a 90:10 general hard-intervention funding pattern, a 95:5 NER/J&K/hilly pattern, and exclusion of land cost from scheme funding.
- SFURTI remains a cluster/institution-led route; project funding and dashboard totals are not individual artisan entitlements.
