Editorial findings:

- SBFAS is directly relevant to Indian shipyards and qualifying vessel contracts, not ordinary logistics operators or merchant-ship owners without an eligible construction contract.
- The modified guideline covers eligible agreements from 24 September 2025 and approval through 31 March 2036, with an application step within 45 days of signing the contract.
- Schedule IV describes separate vessel-category rates: 15% for the first INR 100 crore for normal vessels, 20% on the remainder for large normal vessels, and 25% on the remainder for specialized vessels.
- Schedule V ties support to domestic content: below 30% receives nil, 30% to below 40% is pro-rata, and 40% or more receives full support subject to the other conditions.
- India-based hull construction, machinery installation, commissioning and trials, Chartered Accountant domestic-content evidence, price re-evaluation and final-tranche conditions are material publication requirements.
- The record preserves the SBFAP/SBFAS transition and duplicate-support boundaries rather than treating the published rate as an unconditional subsidy.

The record remains review-gated until the live portal, Schedule I exclusions, vessel category, transition terms, price basis, application deadline and claim/recovery route are manually confirmed.
