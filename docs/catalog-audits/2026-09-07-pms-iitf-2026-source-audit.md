## Reconciliation recorded

- The generic Procurement and Marketing Support record remains component-specific and rolling; its illustrative reimbursement terms are not presented as a universal entitlement.
- IITF 2026 is represented separately as an event opportunity so its dated window is not applied to every PMS component.
- IITF 2026 applications are recorded as open from 1 September 2026 through 20 September 2026.
- The event record requires valid Udyam registration as of 31 August 2026.
- Late applications are recorded as not considered.
- Stall fee or an invoice is recorded as not required at application stage and collected only from MSEs allotted stalls, subject to the scheme terms.
- Event application and allotment are not represented as guaranteed participation or guaranteed reimbursement.
