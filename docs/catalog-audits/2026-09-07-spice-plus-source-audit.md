## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- SPICe+ Part A covers name reservation; Part B covers the remaining incorporation details. Part A may be submitted alone for name reservation or together with Part B.
- The official FAQ describes AGILE-PRO-S as the linked route for GSTIN where applied, EPFO, ESIC, professional tax in Maharashtra/Karnataka/West Bengal, bank-account opening and Shops and Establishment registration for Delhi.
- New companies are required to apply for a bank account through the linked AGILE-PRO-S route. GSTIN remains optional in that route and must not be treated as universally applicable.
- EPFO and ESIC registration is described as mandatory for new companies through SPICe+, but substantive compliance is still threshold- and applicability-dependent; the FAQ also notes a non-implemented-area exception for ESIC.
- Current MCA notices instruct users to use the V3 environment and not rely on legacy V2 links for current filing work.
- UdyamVeda may prepare information and documents, but the founders or authorised professionals must use their own MCA account, DSC/signing credentials and declarations.
