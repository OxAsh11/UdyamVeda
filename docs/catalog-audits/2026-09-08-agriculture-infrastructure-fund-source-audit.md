# Agriculture Infrastructure Fund source audit

Audit date: 08 September 2026

## Official evidence inspected

- [Agriculture Infrastructure Fund revised scheme guidelines, September 2024](https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in/Documents/Circular/91EE46A50D3941908F192725717E431B.pdf), issued by the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
- The guideline identifies the financing facility as medium- to long-term debt support for viable post-harvest management infrastructure and viable farming assets, and expressly names agriculture entrepreneurs and startups among eligible beneficiary categories.
- The guideline states 3% annual interest subvention up to a ₹2 crore loan limit for a maximum of seven years, and describes credit-guarantee coverage for eligible borrowers up to the same loan limit subject to the stated route.
- The guideline lists post-harvest and farming-asset examples including warehouses, cold chains, packaging, logistics, primary processing, CBG, precision agriculture, nurseries, seed processing, farm automation, hydroponic, mushroom, vertical, aeroponic and protected farming. It also records that standalone secondary processing is not automatically eligible.
- The guideline states an operational period through 2032-33, a loan-disbursement completion boundary at the end of FY 2025-26 in that document, a maximum repayment period of seven years including a moratorium of up to two years, and a private-sector project-count boundary of up to 25 projects subject to location and other conditions.

## Record interpretation

The record keeps the loan, interest-subvention and credit-guarantee values in separate benefit buckets. It does not add the loan ceiling to a grant or subsidy amount. It also treats lender sanction, current portal operation, state/PMU implementation, project classification and document evidence as dependencies rather than assumed eligibility.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and review-gated. Before publication, a reviewer must reconcile any later amendment, current lender route, current sanction/disbursement boundary, project and location rules, LGD treatment, convergence, credit-guarantee mechanics and the current application workflow.
