Editorial findings:

- The Ministry describes a specialty-steel PLI framework with a stated overall budget of INR 6,322 crore and five broad product categories; later PLI 1.2 material adds advanced sub-categories.
- Official annual-report and sector-overview material says PLI 1.2 launched on 4 November 2025 and describes selected-company MoUs, five years of incentives, and current implementation/disbursement activity.
- The current page describes concluded rounds and selected companies. No new-applicant invitation was evidenced in the sources checked, so the record is closed for new discovery until a current invitation is confirmed.
- Incentives are performance-linked to approved investment, production, sales and other commitments. Scheme outlay, committed investment and capacity are not applicant benefit ceilings.
- The 2021 guidelines and later annual-report material state different scheme/incentive-release periods. Both are preserved as source evidence and require human reconciliation before publication.

The record is intentionally review-gated until a human reviewer verifies the controlling PLI 1.2 notice, product list, rates, thresholds, MoU terms, claim route and current window.
