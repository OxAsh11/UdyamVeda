## Reconciliation recorded

- Ayushman Sahakar is cooperative project finance, not a universal healthcare grant.
- The current NCDC scheme index and common loan application route confirm an activity/purpose and loan-type-specific handoff.
- The scheme document records an eight-year loan period with one-to-two years' principal moratorium, conditional 1% term-loan interest reduction for women-majority cooperatives with timely repayment, direct infrastructure funding up to 70% with minimum 30% society share, and State-route funding up to 90% at the NCDC-to-State level.
- Security, appraisal, statutory permissions and repayment remain conditions. Subsidy/grant/VGF convergence is possible only if separately approved under the applicable programme.
