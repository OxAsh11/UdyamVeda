## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- First, second and third loan tranches are up to ₹15,000, ₹25,000 and ₹50,000 respectively.
- Current repayment periods are 12, 18 and 36 months respectively.
- The lending period is stated to continue until 31 March 2030.
- Coverage is being expanded beyond statutory towns to census towns, urban agglomerations and peri-urban areas in a graded manner.
- The ₹30,000 UPI-linked RuPay credit-card ceiling, up to ₹1,600 digital cashback and ₹7,332 crore scheme outlay are kept in separate benefit buckets; none is represented as a guaranteed applicant payout.
