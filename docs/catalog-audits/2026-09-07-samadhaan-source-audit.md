## Reconciliation recorded

- The record remains assistance for delayed-payment recovery through the statutory MSEFC route; it is not a grant, loan, subsidy, tax benefit or guaranteed recovery amount.
- The Ministry material describes the relevant delayed-payment trigger as payment beyond 45 days and states that a filed complaint is required to be registered by the concerned MSEFC within 15 days.
- The Ministry's 2025-26 Annual Report states that new delayed-payment cases are being filed on the MSME ODR Portal from 15 October 2025 and that the ODR portal is being integrated with Samadhaan.
- New applicant handoff is therefore represented as ODR-first with Samadhaan integration, subject to a live portal check before any recommendation or application handoff.
- The ODR guideline's 15-day pre-MSEFC process and possible 15-day consent-based continuation are stored separately from the Ministry Annual Report's 15-day MSEFC registration statement; they must not be presented as one deadline or as a guaranteed resolution timeline.
- Eligibility remains limited to a micro or small enterprise with valid Udyam registration and a delayed-payment claim; this record does not silently decide legal limitation, merits, interest calculation or State MSEFC jurisdiction.
