## Evidence interpretation

- Startup Runway is a public-procurement and government-buyer market-access route, not a grant, subsidy, loan, guaranteed order or guaranteed revenue amount.
- The GeM page identifies dedicated innovative-product and service categories, while the Startup India and Playbook material describes seller registration, startup visibility and conditional relaxations from prior experience, prior turnover and EMD requirements.
- The current Startup India page specifically describes DPIIT-recognised startup seller registration and the Startup India badge. The June 2026 Playbook also describes other innovative entities and an all-startup listing context, while other official GeM/PIB descriptions describe the dedicated marketplace more broadly. This distinction must be resolved by current GeM seller rules and category/tender conditions.
- Relaxations are not universal: the applicable tender, product/service category, quality and technical requirements, buyer conditions and procurement rule control each opportunity. State procurement rules are separate from Central Ministries, Departments and CPSE orders.
