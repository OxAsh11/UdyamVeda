## Confirmed facts captured in the seed dossier

- Every Food Business Operator requires the applicable FSSAI registration or licence route under Section 31 of the FSS Act.
- The 2026 amendment FAQ states that, for covered new applications from 1 April 2026, registration applies up to INR 1.5 crore, a State licence applies above INR 1.5 crore and up to INR 50 crore, and a Central licence applies above INR 50 crore.
- Route selection is not determined by turnover alone: the FoSCoS matrix also uses the kind of business, capacity, location, multi-state activity and other activity-specific conditions.
- The 2026 FAQ states that licences and registrations have perpetual validity unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered; continuing hygiene and food-safety obligations remain.
- Existing businesses may require migration through FoSCoS to align with revised thresholds. UdyamVeda must not represent a migration or official application as completed.
