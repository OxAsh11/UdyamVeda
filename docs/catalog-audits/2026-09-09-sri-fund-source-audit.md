# SRI Fund source audit

Date: 9 September 2026

## Finding

The Startup India June 2026 Playbook identifies the Self-Reliant India Fund as an equity/quasi-equity fund-of-funds route for viable MSMEs and startup MSMEs. The primary operational source is the official NVCFL scheme page, cross-checked with the NVCFL home and daughter-fund route plus Ministry of MSME annual-report material.

SRI is not a direct founder grant, subsidy or government loan. It is a Category-II mother fund that supports empanelled daughter funds, which then evaluate and invest in eligible MSMEs. The commonly cited INR 10,000 crore government provision and approximately INR 50,000 crore leveraged structure are fund-level figures and must not be shown as an individual applicant benefit.

## Catalog treatment

- Structured record: `self-reliant-india-sri-fund-msme-growth-capital`
- Status: `paused` / `in_review`
- Benefit: indirect equity or permitted quasi-equity investment through daughter funds
- Applicant route: MSME approaches a suitable empanelled daughter fund; NVCFL's interest form is for daughter-fund empanelment
- Exclusions: non-profit institutions, NBFCs, financial-inclusion entities, micro-credit entities, SHGs and other financial intermediaries, subject to current guidelines
- Publication: blocked pending human source, eligibility, current-intake, amendment, instrument-term and factual review
