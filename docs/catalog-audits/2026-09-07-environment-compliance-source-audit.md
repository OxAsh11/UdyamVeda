# MoEFCC/CPCB environmental compliance source audit

Date: 2026-09-07

This batch adds three review-gated Central records:

- CPCB plastic-packaging EPR registration
- CPCB e-waste EPR registration
- MoEFCC environmental clearance through PARIVESH

The evidence set uses the official [CPCB plastic EPR SOP](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/plasticwaste/SOP_PWM_24062024.pdf), [CPCB e-waste FAQ](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/E-Waste/FAQ_ewaste_23012024.pdf), [CPCB rules/EPR index](https://cpcb.nic.in/rules-7/), [MoEFCC EIA overview](https://moef.gov.in/index.php/environmental-impact-assessment-eia), [PARIVESH](https://parivesh.nic.in/) and the [MoEFCC Citizen Charter](https://moef.gov.in/uploads/2024/05/iv-Citizen-Charter-MoEFCC.pdf).

## Editorial boundaries

- EPR registration is a compliance obligation, not a grant, subsidy or financial benefit.
- Plastic and e-waste registration, targets, annual returns and certificates remain separate conditions.
- PARIVESH is a project-level approval workflow; submission does not guarantee clearance, and other permissions may remain separate.

## Human review gate

All three records remain `in_review`, with unreviewed structured rules and open publication tasks. Human review must reconcile current rule text, portal fields, category thresholds, documents, fees, deadlines, target calculations, site/project dependencies and amendments before approval.
