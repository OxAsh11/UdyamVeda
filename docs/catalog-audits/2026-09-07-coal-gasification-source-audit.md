## Captured boundaries

The current RFP accepts PSUs, private companies, JVs and consortia of no more than three companies for new surface coal/lignite gasification projects. It requires at least 0.5 MTPA clean syngas capacity and applicant net worth above 20% of project cost.

The financial incentive is capped at 20% of eligible Plant and Machinery cost, with a single-project ceiling of INR 500 crore. It is a competitive, project-level incentive paid in four milestone-linked instalments; the total scheme outlay and caps are not an individual MSME entitlement.

The Round-1 timeline states 07 September 2026 as the application due date. The Ministry notice specifies online submission plus applicable physical documents by 5:00 PM IST, while the RFP says timelines are tentative and subject to official change.
