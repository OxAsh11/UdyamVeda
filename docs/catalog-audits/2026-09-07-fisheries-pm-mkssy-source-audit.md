Editorial findings:

- The record targets fishers, fish farmers, fish workers, fish vendors, startups and fisheries micro/small enterprises directly engaged in the fisheries value chain.
- NFDP registration is a prerequisite for the modeled route; self-registration or assisted CSC registration is supported by the official framework.
- Formalisation, working-capital access, aquaculture-insurance incentive, performance grants and safety/quality support are separate benefit buckets.
- Performance grants require qualifying investment, measurable outcomes and post-verification; no universal grant percentage or ceiling is inferred.
- The cited framework describes insurance-incentive caps and a limited support amount for successful bankable-loan proposal approval; these are component-specific and are not combined into one “eligible value.”
- The cited scheme period runs through FY 2026-27. Current component availability, application deadlines and any continuation require reviewer confirmation.
