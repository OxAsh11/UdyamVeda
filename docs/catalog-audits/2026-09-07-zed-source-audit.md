## Reconciled facts

- The DC MSME page describes Bronze, Silver and Gold certification levels and lists fee examples of Rs 10,000, Rs 40,000 and Rs 90,000.
- It describes a Rs 10,000 joining reward, differentiated certification-cost subsidy rates of 80%, 60% and 50% for micro, small and medium enterprises, and additional categories subject to the published conditions.
- It says manufacturing MSMEs registered on the Udyam portal are eligible to participate and identifies Quality Council of India as implementing agency.
- Testing/quality/product certification, handholding and technology-upgradation support are separate support categories and are not one direct cash benefit.
