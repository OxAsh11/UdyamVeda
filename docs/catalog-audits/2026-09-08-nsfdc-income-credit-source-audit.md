## Reconciliation recorded

- The current NSFDC FAQ, updated 8 September 2026, states SC eligibility and an annual family-income ceiling of INR 5 lakh for loan assistance, effective 7 January 2026 and subject to amendment.
- NSFDC does not entertain direct loan applications. Applications route through authorized SCAs or other CAs, with PM-SURAJ identified as an online route and local channel partners handling offline applications.
- The current FAQ states up to 90% of project cost across schemes. The product tracks must remain separate: MFS (up to INR 1.40 lakh project cost / INR 1.25 lakh loan / 6.5%), AMY (same project-cost and loan ceiling / 15%), Term Loan (above INR 1.40 lakh to INR 50 lakh project cost / INR 45 lakh loan / 8%), and Udyam Nidhi (up to INR 5 lakh project cost / INR 4.50 lakh loan / 13% or 15% depending on channel).
- Current moratorium and repayment terms are product-specific. Skill-development and market-linkage support are separate non-loan activities.
- Older compendium values remain historical cross-check evidence; the current FAQ controls where values differ.
