## Reconciliation recorded

- ADEETIE supports Udyam-registered MSMEs operating in identified energy-intensive clusters and 14 listed sectors. The current BEE page records an initial 60-cluster implementation scope.
- BEE records 5% interest subvention for Micro and Small Enterprises and 3% for Medium Enterprises. Eligible loan amounts are ₹10 lakh to ₹15 crore, with up to 75% project-cost debt funding qualifying for interest subvention.
- Projects must achieve and sustain at least 10% energy savings during the scheme period. BEE also describes investment-grade energy audits and DPR reimbursement up to ₹75,000 for Micro/Small and ₹1,00,000 for Medium enterprises per project/loan.
- The PIB release records a ₹1,000 crore programme outlay, split into ₹875 crore interest subvention, ₹50 crore audit support and ₹75 crore handholding support. That programme allocation is not an individual entitlement and is not added to the applicant's benefit estimate.
- The implementation period is FY 2025-26 through FY 2027-28; EOI, cluster, lender and claim availability may be phased.
