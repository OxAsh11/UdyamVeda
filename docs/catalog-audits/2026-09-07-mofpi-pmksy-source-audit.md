# MoFPI PMKSY source audit — 2026-09-07

## Scope

This batch adds three Central Government opportunity drafts from the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI). It deliberately separates PMKSY components and does not duplicate the existing PMFME micro-food-processing record.

## Records

| Record | Route captured | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure | Cold storage, pack house, pre-cooling, transport, value addition and irradiation infrastructure | Project-level grant. The 17:00, 30 September 2026 deadline applies only to the specified multiproduct food irradiation EOI. |
| Creation/Expansion of Food Processing and Preservation Capacities (CEFPPC) | New, modernised or expanded food-processing and preservation units | Project-level EOI route; the ₹5 crore ceiling is not an assured individual payout. |
| Creation of Infrastructure for Agro Processing Clusters (APC) | Common enabling and core processing infrastructure linking producers, processors and markets | Cluster/common-infrastructure route; the ₹10 crore ceiling is not an individual-unit entitlement. |

## Evidence interpretation

- The MoFPI FAQ describes APC assistance at 35%/50% of eligible project cost up to ₹10 crore and CEFPPC assistance at 35%/50% up to ₹5 crore. These are project-level ceilings subject to current guidelines, EOI selection and eligible-cost rules.
- The Cold Chain pattern page differentiates storage, value-addition and irradiation rates and describes staged releases. The record keeps those rates component-specific and does not convert them into a single “benefit value.”
- MoFPI describes CEFPPC and APC proposals as EOI-based. Current project-status notices are not treated as proof that a fresh EOI is open.
- The August 2026 irradiation notice extends the specified EOI to 17:00 on 30 September 2026. The date is not copied to general Cold Chain, CEFPPC or APC routes.
