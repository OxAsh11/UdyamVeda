## Reconciliation recorded

- This is an SHG-member micro-finance route implemented through State Channelizing Agencies and partners, with a predominant minority-women focus. It is not a direct individual business-loan checkout.
- The current NMDFC schemes page separates Credit Line 1 at annual family income up to INR 3 lakh and Credit Line 2 at up to INR 8 lakh. It states up to INR 1 lakh per member / INR 20 lakh per SHG for Credit Line 1 and up to INR 1.5 lakh per member / INR 30 lakh per SHG for Credit Line 2, with an SHG size of up to 20 members.
- The current summary states 7% and 10% beneficiary rates, a 2% women concession under Credit Line 2, a 3-month moratorium and a 3-year repayment period. These values remain subject to the controlling guideline and SCA agreement.
- Older Ministry/NMDFC material contains lower historical micro-finance values, including INR 25,000 per beneficiary. That conflict is retained for reviewer reconciliation; the current NMDFC page is not silently merged with the older value.
- Member and group ceilings must remain separate in recommendations. They must not be added into a personal benefit amount or presented as a guaranteed sanction.
