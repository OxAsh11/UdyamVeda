# Chemicals Promotion and Development Scheme source audit

Audit date: 08 September 2026

## Official evidence inspected

- [Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals CPDS page](https://chemicals.gov.in/cpds), official Government of India source.
- The page identifies the scheme as the Chemical Promotion Development Scheme and links the funding and execution guidelines, event-support conditions, general guidelines, and training guidance.
- The page states that CPDS has been implemented in the Department's Chemical Division since 1997 and records revisions in 2017 and 2019.
- The stated objectives cover knowledge products, studies, surveys, data banks, promotional material, seminars, workshops, conferences, exhibitions, training, investor meetings, and research and innovation recognition.
- The page presents four components: creation of knowledge products, knowledge dissemination, excellence awards for research and innovation, and other approved activities.

## Applicant and benefit interpretation

- The page describes grants-in-aid for eligible organisations, industry associations, and similar bodies undertaking approved sector-promotion and development activities.
- This is not evidence of a universal direct subsidy, loan ceiling, or individual-company entitlement.
- A business may benefit indirectly through an approved association or sector activity, but that must not be represented as an individual grant approval.

## Application and fine-print boundary

- The page indicates a web-based CPDS application route and NGO-DARPAN integration for grants-in-aid proposals.
- The current call/window, eligible applicant classes, maximum assistance, eligible-cost rules, sanction terms, release schedule, utilisation reporting, audit requirements, and recovery conditions require reconciliation against the linked guidelines and current application portal.

## Reviewer decision

Keep the record `in_review` and publication-blocked. Preserve `direct_company_subsidy: false` and classify the opportunity as association-led sector-development support until a reviewer verifies the linked guidelines and current call status. This audit confirms the official source identity and broad scope; it does not establish that a new call is open.
