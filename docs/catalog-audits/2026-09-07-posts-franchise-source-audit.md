# India Post franchise source audit - 7 September 2026

Status: two `active` market-access records with `in_review` versions; both are local selection routes and income is not guaranteed.

## Sources checked

- [Current India Post Franchise Scheme page](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/vacancies/franchise) - current service scope and application/selection route.
- [India Post Franchise Scheme 2021](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/vacancies/Franchise_2021.pdf) - franchise-outlet and postal-agent eligibility, security, commission, licence and operating conditions.
- [India Post business services](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/business-services/otherservices) - current commercial-services navigation and franchise entry point.

## Reconciliation recorded

- Franchise outlets provide defined counter services; delivery and transmission remain with the Department. Eligible applicants include individuals, local shops, institutions and other entities in urban/rural and specified growth locations.
- The scheme document records individual age above 18, 8th-class qualification, suitable premises, a business plan, local reference and a minimum ₹5,000 NSC security deposit for franchise outlets. Selection is by the concerned Divisional authority and requires a Department agreement.
- Postal agents are a separate, narrower route for stamps and stationery. The document records age above 18, no prescribed educational qualification, no security deposit, a minimum ₹300 cash purchase transaction and 5% commission/deduction subject to availability. The initial licence is one year, with later continuation subject to satisfactory work and conduct.
- Family members of serving postal employees are excluded in the same Division for both routes. Local availability, service permissions and current terms must be checked with the concerned Postal Division.

## Human review gate

- Confirm the current form, local divisional availability and whether the 2021 document has been amended.
- Verify the PSSK exclusion, premises requirements, security calculation, service/PLI/e-governance permissions, commission schedule and continuation/termination terms.
- Keep franchise-outlet and postal-agent routes separate in recommendations and value estimates.
- Do not present commission schedules as fixed income or a selected route as a guaranteed business opportunity.
