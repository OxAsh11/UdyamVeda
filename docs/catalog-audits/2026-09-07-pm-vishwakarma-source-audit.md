## Reconciliation recorded

- The current portal continues to describe 18 family-based traditional trades, minimum age 18, self-employment in the unorganised sector, the five-year similar-loan restriction, one-member-per-family rule and government-service exclusion.
- Current FAQ guidance identifies Aadhaar, mobile number, bank details and ration-card/family information for registration; the exact live CSC workflow remains a reviewer-controlled checklist.
- Benefits remain separate: recognition, training, toolkit incentive, credit, digital-transaction incentive and market support. They are not added into one guaranteed value.
- The toolkit incentive is tied to skill verification at the start of Basic Training. The second credit tranche has additional skilled-beneficiary and repayment/digital-training conditions.
- The current portal shows a registration target through 2028. This is stored as a current portal fact, not treated as an automatic amendment to the underlying guideline until reviewed.
- PM Vishwakarma remains a rolling opportunity with live component availability and multi-stage verification; registration alone is not represented as benefit approval.
