- [iDEX ADITI](https://idex.gov.in/disc-category/26) for critical and strategic defence technologies.

## Editorial boundaries

- ADITI and TDF are distinct routes; their grant ceilings, entity rules, technology scope and calls must not be combined.
- The ADITI page currently displays a historical ADITI 1 timeline and no current challenge; the record is paused.
- The TDF portal currently displays no projects available at this stage; the record is paused.
- Grant percentages and ceilings are reference values subject to a current call, approved budget, agreement and milestones.
- Selection, testing, user acceptance, certification and procurement are separate decisions.

## Human review gate

Before publication, verify the live challenge/project, deadline, applicant ownership, startup/MSME evidence, project definition, DPR, cost ceiling, matching contribution, milestone agreement, testing, security, IP, procurement and any amendment. UdyamVeda prepares and explains the route; it does not impersonate DIO/DRDO or submit an application as the user.
