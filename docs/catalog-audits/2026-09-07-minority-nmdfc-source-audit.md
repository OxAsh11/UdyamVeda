# Ministry of Minority Affairs / NMDFC credit-route source audit

Audit date: 2026-09-07

This batch adds three separate Central opportunity dossiers, all kept in `in_review`:

1. NMDFC Term Loan Scheme for Employment-Generating Ventures
2. NMDFC Micro-Finance Scheme for Minority SHGs
3. NMDFC Virasat Credit Scheme for Craftpersons

## Official sources reviewed

- NMDFC current schemes: https://www.nmdfc.org/nmdfcschemes
- NMDFC current FAQ: https://www.nmdfc.org/faq
- NMDFC Credit Line 1 detail: https://www.nmdfc.org/credit-1
- NMDFC micro-finance guidelines: https://www.nmdfc.org/guidelines_for_micro_financing_schemes
- NMDFC current scheme landing page: https://www.nmdfc.org/home/nmdfc_schemes
- Ministry of Minority Affairs NMDFC booklet: https://minorityaffairs.gov.in/WriteReadData/RTF1984/1953017398.pdf

## Controlled interpretation

- The Term Loan, Micro-Finance and Virasat routes are separate products with different borrowers, ceilings, rates, contribution and repayment conditions.
- The current NMDFC schemes page describes two income-based credit lines: up to INR 3 lakh annual family income for Credit Line 1 and up to INR 8 lakh for Credit Line 2.
- Term Loan is for viable individual employment-generating ventures; Micro-Finance is for eligible SHG members; Virasat is for minority craftpersons/artisans' working and fixed capital.
- Current pages describe route-specific ceilings, including Term Loan up to INR 20 lakh/INR 30 lakh by credit line, Micro-Finance member/group ceilings, and Virasat up to INR 10 lakh. These must not be combined into one user benefit.
- Applications are routed through State Channelizing Agencies and/or banking partners. Eligibility is not a guarantee of sanction.

## Human review gate

Neither record nor route is publishable from this audit alone. A reviewer must reconcile the current NMDFC pages, FAQ, lending policy, micro-finance guideline, SCA availability, forms, contribution, security, rates, repayment, amendments and current document requirements before approving a version.
