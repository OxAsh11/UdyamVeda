# SITARE source audit — 2026-09-08

## Decision

SITARE is resolved from the Startup India Playbook lead into a structured, paused and review-gated record. It is a student-facing biotechnology innovation route, not a general incorporated-company, MSME or startup grant.

## First-party sources reviewed

- [BIRAC SITARE programme page](https://www.birac.nic.in/desc_new.php?id=581): GYTI, BIIS, Appreciation, eligibility, annual timing and active-call route.
- [SITARE scheme guidelines](https://www.birac.nic.in/webcontent/1568955483_birac_sitare_gyti_biis_guidelines.pdf): component boundaries, project duration, milestones, selection and terms.
- [BIRAC supported projects](https://www.birac.nic.in/birac_supported_projects.php): historical SITARE-GYTI results route; not evidence of a current intake.
- [BIRAC current calls](https://www.birac.nic.in/cfp.php): checked for a live SITARE call on 2026-09-08; no current SITARE call was established by the reviewed listing.
- [BIRAC proposal submission guide](https://www.birac.nic.in/howtoSubmitProposal.php): official account, proposal and submission guidance.

## Structured facts retained

- SITARE-GYTI: up to 15 selected projects, up to INR 15 lakh each, for up to 24 months, subject to selection, milestones and agreement terms.
- BIIS: student training and mentoring route with institution affiliation and the applicable workshop process.
- SITARE-Appreciation: separate awards described as up to INR 1 lakh for selected BIIS work.
- GYTI requires the student route, institutional identity and prescribed NoC; proposals must be translational biotechnology work with startup potential.
- The current call or notified BIIS route controls whether an application can be made. Historical supported projects and annual timing do not create a live deadline.

## Publication gate

The record remains `paused`, `in_review`, and `publicationAllowed: false` until a reviewer verifies the current component, call, dates, applicant route, amendments, student definitions, budget/milestone terms, IP/reporting obligations and partner instructions. Benefit buckets stay separate; the INR 15 lakh and INR 1 lakh figures are not combined or presented as a company entitlement.
