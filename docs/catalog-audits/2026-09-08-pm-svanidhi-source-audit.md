# PM SVANidhi source audit — 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers PM Street Vendor’s AtmaNirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi), a working-capital credit route for eligible street vendors. It must not be represented as a guaranteed grant, a guaranteed loan sanction, or a general MSME loan.

## Official evidence reviewed

- PIB current tranche and repayment clarification, 27 July 2026: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2289997&lang=1&reg=48
- PIB restructuring and extension release, 27 August 2025: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2161157&lang=1&reg=1
- PM SVANidhi official portal disclaimer: https://www.disclaimerpms.mohua.gov.in/
- PM SVANidhi application portal referenced by PIB: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in/

## Evidence interpretation

- Current PIB clarification describes three working-capital loan tranches of up to ₹15,000, ₹25,000 and ₹50,000 with repayment periods of 12, 18 and 36 months respectively.
- The 2025 restructuring release states an extension of the lending period to 31 March 2030, higher first and second tranche ceilings, digital cashback, UPI-linked credit-card support and expanded coverage beyond statutory towns in a graded manner.
- The portal disclaimer makes the lender boundary explicit: submission is not an assurance of sanction, and lenders apply their own credit and risk policies.
- SVANidhi se Samriddhi and socio-economic convergence should be modeled as a separate non-loan support component, not added to the credit ceiling.
