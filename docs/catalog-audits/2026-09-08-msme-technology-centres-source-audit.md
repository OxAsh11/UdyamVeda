# MSME Tool Rooms and Technology Centres source audit - 2026-09-08

## Scope

This audit covers access to Ministry of MSME Tool Rooms and Technology Centres, including technical services, training, consultancy, job work and current Technology Centre or Extension Centre network context. Institutional construction targets are not applicant-level grants and must not be presented as direct cash benefits.

## Official evidence reviewed

- Ministry of MSME Technology Centre and Extension Centre dashboard: https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/100_TC_EC.aspx
- Ministry of MSME Tool Room and Technology Centre dashboard: https://www.dashboard.msme.gov.in/tcsp_details.aspx
- MyMSME official scheme booklet, version 2.0, 14 February 2025: https://my.msme.gov.in/files/assets/pdf/v2.0%20-2025-%20Scheme%20booklet%20Eng-14.02.2025.pdf
- Existing primary source retained for the related Technology Centre Systems Programme record: https://dcmsme.gov.in/CLCS_TUS_Scheme/Tool_Room_Tech_Institutions/Scheme_Guidelines.aspx

## Evidence interpretation

- The scheme booklet describes 18 Tool Rooms and Technical Institutions, generally known as Technology Centres, with tooling, training, product/process development, consultancy and job-work support.
- The listed route includes industrial units, with a focus on MSMEs, and training participants from school-dropout level through M.Tech level; course, centre, fee, schedule and capacity rules are centre-specific.
- The current Ministry dashboard reports a 20 Technology Centre and 100 Extension Centre hub-and-spoke expansion context and separately reports current Technology Centre Systems Programme infrastructure status.
- These network and construction figures are institutional context. They do not create an automatic grant, subsidy or guaranteed service entitlement for every business.

## Human review gate

The record remains `in_review` and publication-blocked until a reviewer reconciles the current centre directory, centre-specific service and course routes, fees, admissions, capacity, documents, geographic availability, and any revised TCSP or Extension Centre terms.
