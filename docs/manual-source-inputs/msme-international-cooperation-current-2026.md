## Current facts for structured review

- The scheme builds MSME export capacity through international exhibitions,
  fairs, buyer-seller meets, conferences and related market intelligence or
  first-time-exporter support.
- Eligibility and benefits are component-specific. A business must not be
  shown one universal reimbursement amount for the entire IC scheme.
- The current displayed call covered events from 1 June through 30 September
  2026, but the 5 May 2026 submission deadline has passed as of this audit.
- The portal separates applicant types/routes, including MSME and
  government/industry-association/society/trust paths. The current form and
  call determine which route applies.
- Supporting evidence may include Udyam/MSME identity, event details, cost or
  reimbursement records, export-readiness information and the documents
  requested by the selected component.
- Assistance, eligible costs, caps, reimbursement/advance treatment and
  selection vary by component and call.

## Editorial boundaries

Set the current 2026 call window to `closed`; retain the scheme as a recurring

the 2026 notice as actionable after 5 May 2026. Do not combine exhibition,
