## Source anchors

Guidelines, sections 1–3: MSME Innovative combines Incubation, Design and IPR
as separate but interoperable verticals and aims to move ideas toward
commercialisation.

Guidelines, section 4: Incubation, Design and IPR have different activities,
applicants, implementing institutions and benefits.

Guidelines, section 7: an MSME may choose one component or a permitted
combination; assistance follows the individual component rules and one
component’s completion does not erase the next component’s separate criteria.

Current portal pages: Design advertises up to ₹40 lakh assistance subject to
conditions; the IPR page exposes patent, GI, trademark and design-patent
reimbursement routes; the Incubation page exposes Host Institute and Idea
Hackathon routes.

Current incubation page checked on 8 September 2026: the Host Institute window
for forwarding recommended ideas under Idea Hackathon 6.0 was shown as extended
through 7 September 2026. That displayed deadline has passed as of this audit.

## Current facts for structured review

- Incubation is primarily Host Institute/Business Incubator-led. Institutions
  such as technical colleges, universities, R&D institutes, eligible NGOs,
  MSME-DIs, Technology Centres and government institutes can apply for HI
  registration; ideas then follow the HI/MIS evaluation route.
- The guideline describes up to ₹15 lakh per idea for nurturing through the
  Host Institute route. This is not a direct universal founder grant.
- Design and IPR are separate component routes. Their contribution,
  reimbursement, eligible-cost and applicant conditions must be evaluated
  independently.
- The current Design page advertises up to ₹40 lakh financial assistance and
  an Udyam-registration path, but the amount must not be presented without
  component-level rules and matching/eligible-cost checks.
- The current IPR page lists separate reimbursement routes for patents, GI,
  trademarks and design patents; exact caps and conditions come from the
  applicable guideline and portal route.
- The guideline treats Incubation, Design and IPR components as separate
  components with their own routes and conditions.
- Idea Hackathon 6.0 and its displayed Host Institute deadline are a specific
  challenge window. They must not be treated as the expiry of all MSME
  Innovative Design/IPR routes.

## Editorial boundaries

Keep MSME Innovative as a component-specific rolling framework with challenge
windows recorded separately. Mark Idea Hackathon 6.0’s displayed 7 September
2026 Host Institute deadline as passed. Do not combine incubation support,
design assistance and IPR reimbursements into one amount. Do not show ₹40 lakh
or ₹15 lakh as a universal founder benefit, and require Udyam/component/route
verification before recommending an application.
