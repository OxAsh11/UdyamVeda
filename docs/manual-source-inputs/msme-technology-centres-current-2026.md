# MSME Technology Centres current evidence audit (2026-09-08)

### Technology Centres; technological and skilled manpower support; technical services, training and business advisory support

The official Development Commissioner (MSME) page describes 18 Technology Centres, including Tool Rooms and Technology Development Centres, that provide technology, technical services, product/process support, training and skilled manpower support to MSMEs. The current official programme pages also describe the Technology Centre Systems Programme and the new Technology Centre/Extension Centre expansion routes.

Current official operational sources:

- Technology Centre and Tool Room service page: https://dcmsme.gov.in/CLCS_TUS_Scheme/Tool_Room_Tech_Institutions/Scheme_Guidelines.aspx
- Technology Centre Systems Programme: https://dcmsme.gov.in/CLCS_TUS_Scheme/TCSP/Scheme_Guidelines.aspx
- New Technology Centres and Extension Centres: https://dcmsme.gov.in/CLCS_TUS_Scheme/new-Technology-Centers/Scheme_Guidelines.aspx
- Current Extension Centre status dashboard: https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/100_TC_EC.aspx

The official pages distinguish institutional establishment targets from business access. The TCSP and new-TC/EC targets are not a direct enterprise grant. Most services are chargeable, and any concession, intake, capacity, documents or turnaround must be verified with the selected centre. Dashboard counts are monitoring context and do not prove a business entitlement or outcome.
