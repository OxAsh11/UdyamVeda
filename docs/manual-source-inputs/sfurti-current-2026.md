## Route boundary

### Objectives of SFURTI Scheme

SFURTI is a cluster and implementing-agency route. The revised guideline describes organising traditional industries and artisans/producers into collectives and supporting common facilities, technology, training, product development, design, packaging and marketability. Eligible routes involve a qualifying cluster and a nodal or implementing agency. An individual artisan, self-employed person or founder should therefore be guided to identify or join a qualifying cluster and its implementing route; the product must not present SFURTI as a direct personal grant application.

The portal exposes a new-proposal entry point, but the current page does not establish a universal deadline or guarantee that every proposal route is open for every cluster. The opportunity record must remain review-gated until the applicable nodal agency, current notice, project terms, funding pattern, project ceiling and live submission state are checked.
