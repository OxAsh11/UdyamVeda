# Manual official-source capture: Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

Inspected: 08 September 2026

Official document: “Scheme Guidelines for Central Sector Scheme of Financing Facility under Agriculture Infrastructure Fund — Revised Scheme Guidelines, September 2024.” The document identifies the Department of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India as the authority.

## Scheme purpose and financing facility

The guideline describes a medium- to long-term debt financing facility for viable post-harvest management infrastructure and viable farming assets. It expressly includes agriculture entrepreneurs and startups, alongside PACS, FPOs, cooperatives, SHGs, APMCs and relevant public or PPP entities.

## Benefit structure

All loans under the financing facility receive interest subvention of 3% per annum up to a loan limit of INR 2 crore, available for a maximum of seven years. For loans above INR 2 crore, the interest subvention remains limited to the INR 2 crore portion. Credit-guarantee coverage is available for eligible borrowers for a loan up to INR 2 crore under the stated CGTMSE route, with the Government paying the coverage fee subject to the guideline conditions.

## Eligible projects

The listed eligible projects include supply-chain services, warehouses and silos, cold stores and cold chains, packaging, assaying, sorting and grading, logistics, ripening chambers, farm-residue and waste-management infrastructure, primary or integrated processing, organic inputs, compressed biogas, bio-stimulants, smart and precision agriculture, nurseries, tissue culture, seed processing, custom hiring centres, farm automation, specified solarisation, cluster supply-chain infrastructure, hydroponic, mushroom, vertical, aeroponic and protected farming.

The document marks standalone secondary processing as ineligible and records additional boundaries for certain activities and applicant types. It states that the scheme is operational from FY 2020-21 to FY 2032-33, with loan disbursement expected to complete by the end of FY 2025-26, and a repayment period of up to seven years including a moratorium of up to two years.

## Fine-print boundaries

Interest subvention is loan support, not a direct grant. The project must be eligible, the lender must participate through the applicable route, and sanction, guarantee coverage, convergence, repayment, project evidence and current portal operation must be verified before an applicant is told that support is available. The document states that an eligible private-sector entity such as an agri-entrepreneur or startup may have up to 25 projects, subject to the location and other guideline conditions.
