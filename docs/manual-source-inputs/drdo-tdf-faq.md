# DRDO Technology Development Fund FAQ — manual official capture

The official TDF FAQ describes the Technology Development Fund route for defence and dual-use development. It states that the development cost across all phases should not exceed INR 10 crore and that the typical project period is two years. The route is intended for eligible Indian companies, including startups and MSMEs, with the applicable resident-Indian ownership and control conditions. Applications are made through the official TDF portal and support is released against approved milestones rather than as an unconditional cash grant.

The FAQ distinguishes project cost from the applicant's benefit and requires the current call, technology requirement, milestone plan, testing, security, intellectual-property and reporting terms to be checked for each project. This capture confirms the project cost, duration, applicant entities, resident Indian control, online application and milestone funding boundaries; it does not approve a current project or establish that a current challenge is open.
