## Eligibility and 18 trades

The scheme is a Central Sector scheme for artisans and craftspeople working
with their hands and tools in 18 identified traditional trades: carpenter,
boat maker, armourer, blacksmith, hammer and tool-kit maker, locksmith,
goldsmith, potter, sculptor/stone breaker, cobbler/shoesmith/footwear artisan,
mason, basket/mat/broom maker or coir weaver, traditional doll and toy maker,
barber, garland maker, washerman, tailor, and fishing-net maker.

The Government description states that the applicant must be at least 18 and
engaged in the concerned trade on the registration date. The current record
also retains the portal-guided constraints for self-employment in the
unorganised sector, the similar-loan restriction, one member per family, and
the exclusion for government employees and their family members. These are
eligibility conditions, not assumptions to be filled silently when facts are
unknown.

## Benefits

The benefit components are separate:

- recognition through a PM Vishwakarma certificate and identity card;
- Basic Training of 5–7 days and Advanced Training of 15 days or more, with
  a Rs.500 per-day training stipend;
- toolkit incentive up to Rs.15,000 through the prescribed voucher route;
- collateral-free enterprise-development credit up to Rs.3 lakh in two
  tranches of Rs.1 lakh and Rs.2 lakh, with the stated 18- and 30-month
  tenures and 5% beneficiary interest subject to the scheme conditions;
- an incentive for eligible digital transactions; and
- marketing support such as quality certification, branding, e-commerce
  linkage, advertising and trade-fair support.

The second credit tranche has additional conditions, including a standard
first-tranche loan account and the prescribed digital-transaction or Advanced
Training condition. The toolkit incentive follows skill assessment at the
start of Basic Training. These benefits must not be combined into one
guaranteed monetary value.

## Verification and implementation period

Enrolment uses the official portal, commonly through a Common Service Centre,
and is followed by multi-stage verification involving the Gram Panchayat or
Urban Local Body, District Implementation Committee and Screening Committee.
Registration alone does not release every benefit. The current portal shows a
registration target through 2028; this is retained as a current portal fact,
not as an automatic amendment to the guideline until the reviewer confirms
the version and implementation-period basis.

The portal and guideline route require current document, training, lender,
verification and local-operational checks before an actionable handoff. UdyamVeda
may prepare and explain the checklist but must not collect OTPs or impersonate
the applicant on the official portal.
