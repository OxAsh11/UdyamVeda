# Ministry of Culture — Tagore Cultural Complexes

Manual source capture prepared from the official Ministry of Culture scheme page, current application form and DPR guidance.

- The scheme supports multi-purpose Tagore Cultural Complexes and upgrades to auditoria/cultural complexes for music, drama, dance, literature, fine arts, exhibitions, seminars, film shows, training, rehearsal and live performance.
- Eligible applicants include State Governments, UT Administrations, eligible government-sponsored bodies, Central Government bodies, universities, municipal corporations, approved agencies and reputed not-for-profit organizations.
- A nonprofit applicant must be registered for at least three years, have a cultural charter and three years of meaningful cultural activity, and be inspected/recommended by an appropriate government agency. It must also meet matching-share and recurring-cost obligations.
- New projects must recognize Rabindranath Tagore in the project name and normally include an auditorium, subject to district/municipal alternatives.
- Central assistance is normally up to INR 15 crore and may exceptionally reach INR 50 crore for outstanding merit and relevance through the additional appraisal route. Assistance is normally limited to 60% of approved cost, or up to 90% for NER projects. The balance is matching share.
- The application requires a feasibility/DPR package, land or allocation evidence, plans, cost estimates, matching-share commitment, executing agency, operation and maintenance plan, accounts, recommendation, bond and bank authorization.
- Assistance is released in two equal instalments. Progress reports, utilization evidence, audited accounts, professional certification, physical inspection and quarterly reporting apply. Completion is required within three years of the first instalment.
- The current page does not state a fixed public deadline; the current Ministry intake, cut-off and appraisal schedule must be verified before actioning.
