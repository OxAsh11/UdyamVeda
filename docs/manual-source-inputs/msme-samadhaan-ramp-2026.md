## Introduction and legal route

The Ministry page says the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development
(MSMED) Act, 2006 contains provisions on delayed payments to Micro and Small
Enterprises in sections 15–24. It states that State Governments establish
Micro and Small Enterprise Facilitation Councils under sections 20 and 21 to
settle disputes after delayed-payment references or filings.

## Nature of assistance

The relevant State MSE Facilitation Council examines a case filed by the MSE
and may issue directions to the buyer for payment of the amount due together
with interest as provided under the MSMED Act, 2006. This is a statutory
dispute and recovery route, not a grant, loan, subsidy or guaranteed recovery
amount.

## Who can apply and current handoff

The Ministry page identifies any micro or small enterprise with valid Udyam
Registration as eligible to apply. Eligibility, buyer/invoice evidence,
limitation and State MSEFC procedure remain facts that must be confirmed for
the particular claim.

The page links both the Samadhaan portal and the MSME ODR route. The current
record keeps the ODR route for new cases as a current operational fact based on
the Ministry's 2025–26 implementation material, while preserving Samadhaan as
the statutory portal handoff. UdyamVeda may organize evidence and explain the
route but must not decide legal merits or impersonate the MSEFC/ODR institution.
