checked on 8 September 2026. This capture is evidence for editorial review; it
does not establish that a new applicant window is currently open.

## Current facts for structured review

- The scheme targets advanced automotive technology products and investment in
  the automotive manufacturing value chain.
- Applicants are companies or group companies incorporated in India in the
  automotive or auto-component manufacturing sector, or qualifying new
  non-automotive investor companies/group companies.
- Eligible products include pre-approved advanced automotive technology
  vehicles and components; MHI may amend the product list over time.
- The original guidelines describe a 60-day application window and require
  financial and supporting documents, audited/statutory-auditor evidence and a
  non-refundable application fee.
- Product approval and domestic-value-addition evidence are material gates.
  The guideline text states a minimum 50% domestic value addition and a
  testing-agency approval path.
- The original guideline states a total indicative scheme outlay of
  ₹25,938 crore. That is a scheme corpus, not an individual applicant ceiling.
- The original guideline contains performance thresholds of ₹125 crore for
  the Champion OEM track and ₹25 crore for the Component Champion track in
  the first year, with year-on-year growth conditions. These amounts must be
  represented as track thresholds, not as guaranteed benefit amounts.
- Approved companies submit annual claims with audited financial statements
  and supporting documents. If an approved applicant is later found ineligible
  or non-compliant, incentive claims can be forfeited or recovered with
  interest.
- The 2023 amendment changes the applicable incentive period to FY 2023-24
  through five consecutive financial years, with disbursement in the following
  financial year. The scheme period alone does not prove that a new application
  window is open in 2026.

## Editorial boundaries

Do not publish this record as an open opportunity without a current official
notice or portal confirmation of an available application route. Keep the
original guidelines, amendments, product lists and current MHI implementation
page as separate versioned evidence. Do not present the ₹25,938 crore outlay,
₹125 crore/₹25 crore thresholds, or incentive percentages as a universal
grant/benefit ceiling. The official portal route, current product list,
approval status, claims timetable, domestic-value-addition method and any
current amendment must be reviewer-confirmed before publication.
