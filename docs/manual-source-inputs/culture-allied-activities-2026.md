# Ministry of Culture — Financial Assistance for Allied Cultural Activities

Manual source capture prepared from the official Ministry of Culture scheme page, guideline PDF and application proforma.

- This is Sub-component 1B under the Scheme of Financial Assistance for Creation of Cultural Infrastructure and supports audio or audio-video equipment for allied cultural spectacles in visitor-heavy spaces and festivals.
- Eligible applicants include not-for-profit organizations engaged in the activity for at least three years and registered as a society, trust or not-for-profit company for at least three years, as well as State Governments, Union Territories and District Authorities.
- A not-for-profit applicant must provide the prescribed District Authority recommendation. The application materials also require NGO Darpan ID, PAN where applicable, project evidence, quotations, accounts and matching-share evidence.
- Maximum assistance is INR 1 crore for audio and INR 1.5 crore for audio plus video, including applicable duties/taxes and five-year operation and maintenance costing. Assistance is restricted to 90% of approved cost; the balance is matching share.
- The scheme is notified through Ministry/National Science Centre channels. The guideline provides a submission route to the Director, National Science Centre, New Delhi; the current notification and cut-off must be verified before actioning.
- The first instalment is 60% and the second is 40% after invoices, progress reports, audited accounts, utilization evidence, matching-share certification and physical inspection.
- Equipment projects must be completed within one year from release of the first instalment. Separate accounts, audit, asset restrictions, noise-pollution compliance, reporting and refund conditions apply.
