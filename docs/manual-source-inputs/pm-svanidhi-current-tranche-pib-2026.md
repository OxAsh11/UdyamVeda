# PM SVANidhi current tranche and repayment clarification

This reviewer-provided manual extraction is alternate official evidence for the PM SVANidhi record. It is bound to the PIB release dated 27 July 2026 and remains publication-blocked pending reconciliation with the historical MoHUA guideline, FAQ, portal and local implementation route.

## Current clarification

The release states that PM SVANidhi working-capital term loans are provided to street vendors in three tranches of up to INR 15,000, INR 25,000, and INR 50,000, repayable over 12, 18, and 36 months respectively through monthly instalments. State-wise details are directed to the PM SVANidhi portal.

The release also describes the SVANidhi se Samriddhi component and reports implementation statistics. These programme totals are impact context, not an applicant entitlement or a guarantee of loan sanction.
