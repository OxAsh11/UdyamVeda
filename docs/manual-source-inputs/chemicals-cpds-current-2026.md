# Chemicals Promotion and Development Scheme current-page evidence

Official Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals CPDS page reviewed for editorial capture on 8 September 2026. This evidence is source-bound and remains blocked pending human publication review.

## Page identity and current status

The official page is titled “CHEMICAL PROMOTION DEVELOPMENT SCHEME (CPDS)” and lists the current CPDS overview together with links for funding and execution guidelines, event-support conditions, general scheme guidelines, and training-programme guidance. The page states that CPDS has been implemented in the Department’s Chemical Division since 1997 and records revisions in 2017 and 2019.

## Objective and components

The page describes support for chemicals and petrochemicals sector development through knowledge products, studies, surveys, data banks, promotional material, seminars, workshops, conferences, exhibitions, training, investor meetings, research and innovation recognition, and other approved industry-facilitation activities.

The page groups the scheme into four components:

1. Creation of knowledge products.
2. Knowledge dissemination.
3. Excellence awards for research and innovation.
4. Other approved industry-facilitation and support activities.

## Applicant and benefit boundary

The page describes soft support in the form of grants-in-aid to eligible organisations, industry associations and similar bodies for approved sector-promotion and development activities. It does not establish a universal direct subsidy, loan ceiling, or individual-company entitlement. A participating business may benefit indirectly through an approved association or sector activity, but that is not equivalent to an individual grant approval.

## Application and review boundary

The page states that a web-based CPDS application and NGO-DARPAN integration exist for grants-in-aid proposals. The current application window, eligible applicant categories, maximum assistance, eligible-cost rules, sanction terms, release schedule, utilisation reporting, audit requirements and recovery conditions must be reconciled against the linked guidelines and current portal before publication.
