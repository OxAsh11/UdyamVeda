# ASPIRE current portal and revised-guideline evidence

Official Ministry of MSME / ASPIRE evidence captured for editorial review on 8 September 2026. This source reconciles the 2022 operational-guideline boundary with current portal availability; it does not approve the opportunity for publication.

## Guidelines for ASPIRE - A Scheme for Promoting Innovation, Rural Industry & Entrepreneurship

The revised operational guideline dated 28 January 2022 covers continuation of ASPIRE for the five-year period 2021-22 to 2025-26 and specifically describes Livelihood Business Incubator (LBI) and Fund of Funds (FoF) routes. The record must not infer post-2025-26 continuation, current assistance ceilings, or a new universal funding entitlement from this document alone.

## Current official portal route

The Ministry portal currently exposes an Apply for New LBI route and a registration form whose visible applicant category is Livelihood Business Incubator (LBI). The portal also provides scheme guidelines, sign-in, MI-list, reports and contact surfaces. The route is institutional: a founder should be matched to an approved/current LBI for incubation rather than shown a direct personal ASPIRE grant application.

The current portal dashboard displays 109 LBIs approved, 82 functional, 1,15,437 beneficiaries skilled, 32,085 beneficiaries suitably employed and 1,006 micro enterprises established/supported at the captured snapshot. These are programme metrics, not applicant entitlement, a grant ceiling, or a guarantee of approval.

## Application evidence

The official LBI application form at https://aspire.msme.gov.in/WriteReadData/DocumentFile/ASPIRE_New_21112023.pdf identifies application sections including the executive summary, need statement, proposed interventions, plan of action, outputs/outcomes, impact, mentor institute, host institute, proposed LBI, responsible head and LBI coordinator. The current form, attachments, institutional approvals, portal fields and submission conditions must be verified before an application action.

The official registration manual at https://aspire.msme.gov.in/WriteReadData/Circular/RegistrationUserManualAspire.pdf covers the LBI/TBI registration and portal-login process. It is operational guidance, not a replacement for the controlling scheme guideline.

## Editorial decision

Keep ASPIRE as a rolling, review-gated, institution-led opportunity with `direct_individual_grant: false`. Recommend it to host institutions, mentor institutions, incubators or eligible rural-enterprise support organisations only after checking the current route. Do not display dashboard counts as benefits. Before publication, reconcile post-2025-26 continuation, current assistance ceilings, eligible institution categories, contribution conditions, document formats and the live submission state.
