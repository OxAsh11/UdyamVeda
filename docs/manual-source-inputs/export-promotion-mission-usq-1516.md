# Export Promotion Mission — manual official capture

The Department of Commerce policy update describes the Export Promotion Mission as a digitally driven framework with total outlay of INR 25,060 crore for FY 2025-26 to FY 2030-31. It operates through two integrated sub-schemes: NIRYAT PROTSAHAN for MSME trade-finance access, including interest subvention, export factoring, collateral guarantees, e-commerce export credit and diversification support; and NIRYAT DISHA for quality and compliance, international branding, packaging, trade fairs, warehousing, logistics, inland transport, trade intelligence and capacity building.

The same official document separately describes the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Exporters, including 100% guarantee coverage to Member Lending Institutions for additional collateral-free credit facilities up to INR 20,000 crore for eligible exporters, including MSMEs. These are framework and rollout facts, not a single direct applicant entitlement. Current implementing notices, lender routes, eligibility, deadlines and claims requirements remain review items.
