# PMFME Scheme Guidelines — official evidence capture

Captured from the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) PMFME
guidelines PDF linked from the official PMFME newsletters page. The capture is
an evidence note for editorial review, not a replacement for the source PDF.

Official discovery page: https://pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/pmfme/newsletters/
Official source: https://www.pmfme.mofpi.gov.in/newsletters/docs/SchemeGuidelines.pdf

## Source locators

- The table of contents identifies section 4.0 as “Individual Category”,
  section 4.1 as the individual-unit benefit, and section 4.2 as the
  individual-unit eligibility criteria (PDF pages 1 and 6).
- Section 1.2 states that the scheme aims to enhance existing individual
  micro-enterprise competitiveness and promote formalisation, while also
  supporting FPOs, SHGs and producer cooperatives (PDF pages 3–4).
- Section 2.0 defines the One District One Product (ODOP) approach. Section
  2.5 says new units are supported only for ODOP products; section 2.3 gives
  preference to existing individual units producing the district ODOP product
  while allowing consideration of other existing micro enterprises (PDF pages
  4–5).
- Section 4.1 says an individual micro food-processing unit may receive a
  credit-linked capital subsidy of 35% of eligible project cost, capped at
  Rs.10.0 lakh per unit. It also requires at least 10% beneficiary
  contribution, with the balance as a bank loan (PDF page 6).
- Section 4.2 requires an existing micro food-processing unit in operation;
  operational evidence may include an electricity bill or evidence of
  inventory, machinery and sales. The enterprise must be unincorporated, have
  fewer than 10 workers, and preferably process the district ODOP product.
  The applicant must own the enterprise; ownership may be proprietary or a
  partnership firm; the applicant must be above 18 and at least VIII-standard
  pass (PDF page 6).
- Section 4.2 limits financial assistance to one person per family, requires
  willingness to formalise, contribute 10% and obtain bank finance, excludes
  land cost from project cost, and limits eligible workshed lease rent to a
  maximum three-year period (PDF page 6).
- Sections 4.3.2–4.3.5 describe ongoing district-level applications,
  Resource Person field verification and due diligence, District Level
  Committee review, DPR preparation, and bank loan submission (PDF page 7).
- Section 5 separates group, FPO, cooperative and SHG components from the
  individual-unit rule set. It describes distinct requirements such as FPO or
  cooperative turnover, SHG seed capital, and group-level contributions (PDF
  pages 8–9).

## Editorial interpretation

The individual-unit recommendation must not silently treat group, SHG, FPO,
common-infrastructure, branding or training benefits as the individual
subsidy. It must separately ask for operating status, legal form, worker count,
ownership, age, education, family-claim status, ODOP context, project cost,
contribution and bank-finance readiness. A current State or district intake
route must be confirmed before displaying an actionable deadline or asserting
that an application is currently open.

The PDF states the original five-year outlay period (2020–21 to 2024–25).
That historical programme-period statement is not treated as proof that a new
application window is open in 2026. Current official notices, amendments and
State/UT implementation status remain a required human review item.
