## Source anchor

Online applications for IITF-2026 are open from 01.09.2026 to 20.09.2026;
valid Udyam registration as on 31.08.2026; late applications not considered.

## Current event facts

The current DCMSME notice states that online applications for IITF 2026 are
open from 1 September 2026 through 20 September 2026. Applications received
after 20 September 2026 will not be considered. It states that only MSEs with
valid Udyam Registration as on 31 August 2026 are eligible.

The event route is the PMS portal's event listing. Stall fee or a receipt or
invoice is not required at the application stage; the notice states that the
stall fee is collected only from MSEs allotted stalls, according to the PMS
guidelines.

## Editorial boundaries

The event deadline and Udyam cut-off belong only to the IITF 2026 record. They
must not be copied into the generic PMS scheme. Application submission does
not equal event selection, stall allotment or reimbursement approval. The
record remains review-gated until the reviewer confirms the live event listing,
the exact application fields, current allotment terms and any last-minute
notice changes.
