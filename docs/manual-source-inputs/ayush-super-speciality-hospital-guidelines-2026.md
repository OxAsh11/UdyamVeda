## Current facts for structured review

- The benefit is interest subsidy, not a general grant or reimbursement.
- The guideline table gives maximum interest-subsidy references of ₹8.15 crore
  for a 50-bed hospital, ₹2.45 crore for a 10-bed hospital and ₹0.98 crore for
  a day-care centre. These are category caps subject to the actual loan,
  project conditions and the scheme rules.
- The guideline describes a subsidy pattern of 100%, 70%, 50%, 40% and 20%
  across up to five years, with the actual applicable schedule depending on
  the project’s sanctioned stage.
- Interest subsidy is limited to eligible construction, equipment and
  machinery financing. The maximum admissible rate is capped at 9% per annum
  or the actual rate, whichever is lower.
- SIDBI channels the subsidy to the financial institution/bank after project
  progress or completion is confirmed under the construction-linked plan and
  required documents/EMI conditions.
- The project must be completed and made operational within a period not
  exceeding three years and the facility must be operated for 15 years from
  initial operation under the cited obligations.
