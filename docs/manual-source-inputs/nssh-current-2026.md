# National SC-ST Hub current guideline and dashboard evidence

Official Ministry of MSME / National SC-ST Hub evidence captured for editorial review on 8 September 2026. This source reconciles the current dashboard with the controlling guideline period; it does not approve the opportunity for publication.

## Definition of SC/ST MSEs and Functions of NSSH

The official revised guideline is dated 15 May 2025 and is expressly for the 15th Financial Cycle, 2021-22 to 2025-26. The guideline covers NSSH functions including procurement participation, capacity building, vendor development, mentoring and credit linkages for eligible SC/ST-owned micro and small enterprises. Ownership thresholds vary by entity form and must be evaluated from the guideline rather than inferred from the dashboard.

The guideline's cited financial cycle ends on 31 March 2026. No post-31 March 2026 successor, extension or universal application window was evidenced in the official sources checked for this capture. The opportunity therefore remains closed nationally until a current operating or continuation notice is verified.

## Dashboard boundary

The official MSME National SC-ST Hub dashboard was last updated 14 August 2026 and includes 2026-27 reporting context, procurement data and intervention reporting. This operational dashboard is evidence that programme activity is being reported; it is not evidence of a new legal cycle, continued funding authority, universal applicant eligibility or a new application window.

The dashboard URL is:

https://dashboard.msme.gov.in/scsthub.aspx

## Editorial decision

Keep NSSH as a closed, review-gated opportunity with the current 31 March 2026 cycle boundary. Do not present dashboard activity as a benefit or as proof that a new application route is open. Before publication, verify a successor/extension notice, current implementing route, SC/ST ownership thresholds, procurement/support components, documents, application process and any post-2025-26 authorization.
