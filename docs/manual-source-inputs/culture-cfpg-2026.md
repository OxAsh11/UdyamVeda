# Ministry of Culture — Cultural Function and Production Grant

Manual source capture prepared from the official Ministry of Culture page and its linked guideline and prescribed-form PDFs.

- The scheme covers not-for-profit organizations, NGOs, societies, trusts and universities for cultural seminars, conferences, research, workshops, festivals, exhibitions, productions and related projects.
- The organization must have been functioning and registered for at least three years; religious institutions, schools, colleges and college/university festivals are excluded.
- Voluntary organizations and NGOs need a properly constituted managing body and NGO Darpan Unique ID; the organization must have facilities, personnel and experience for the proposed project.
- The organization must have tied up or planned matching resources of at least 25% of project cost.
- Assistance is normally restricted to 75% of expenditure and up to INR 5 lakh per project. In exceptional cases, projects of outstanding merit and relevance may receive up to INR 20 lakh with Minister of Culture approval.
- The scheme page says applications are open throughout the year, subject to funds and Expert Committee meetings. Applications use the prescribed form and recommendation route and are sent to the current notified cultural authority.
- The checklist includes registration and governing-body records, annual report, detailed project report, itemized budget, three years of accounts, latest balance sheet, recommendation, indemnity bond, bank authorization and prior utilization records.
- Grant is released in 75% and 25% instalments. Separate accounts, audited statements, utilization evidence, audit access and output/reporting requirements apply.
