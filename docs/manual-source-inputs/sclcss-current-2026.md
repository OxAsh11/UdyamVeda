# SCLCSS current portal and application evidence

Official Ministry of MSME / SCLCSS portal evidence captured for editorial review on 8 September 2026. This source preserves the component terms and current document route while keeping the NSSH financial-cycle boundary explicit.

## Nature of assistance, Who can be benefitted and How to apply

The official SCLCSS portal describes a 25% capital subsidy for eligible SC/ST-owned micro and small enterprises purchasing new plant, machinery or equipment through institutional credit, with an overall subsidy ceiling of Rs. 25 lakh subject to the applicable official conditions. This is a lender-linked subsidy, not a direct cash grant and not an automatic sanction.

The portal describes the application route through the concerned Prime Lending Institution from which the term loan is availed. The applicant must still satisfy the entity-size, SC/ST ownership, asset, lender and documentation conditions.

## Current document route

The portal links an official application checklist dated 26 January 2026:

https://sclcss.msme.gov.in/Manual/Checklist_SCLCSS_Application_26Jan2026.pdf

The checklist references the application form, revised/family undertaking, DIC clearance, PLI due-diligence certificate, GeM undertaking and an appropriately current caste certificate. These are operational document references and do not override the controlling guideline or extend its financial-cycle period.

## Window boundary

SCLCSS is represented as a component under NSSH. The official NSSH revised guideline dated 15 May 2025 cites the 2021-22 to 2025-26 financial cycle, ending 31 March 2026. The current SCLCSS portal and its January 2026 checklist do not, by themselves, evidence a post-31 March 2026 continuation or universal new-application window.

## Editorial decision

Keep SCLCSS closed nationally and review-gated pending an official continuation or operating notice. Preserve the 25% / Rs. 25 lakh figures as cited component terms, separate from the NSSH programme boundary, and never present them as guaranteed benefit value for an applicant.
