# Ayushman Sahakar — official NCDC evidence capture

NCDC’s Ayushman Sahakar scheme document, current NCDC scheme/application
pages and the 2024 English compendium were checked on 8 September 2026. This
is a cooperative-sector financing route, not a universal grant or direct
founder benefit.

## Source anchors

Primary scheme document and 2024 compendium: cooperative eligibility, suitable
bye-law provision, eligible healthcare/AYUSH/education/insurance/digital-health
activities, loan period, moratorium, women-majority interest incentive, funding
patterns, security and subsidy-convergence boundary.

Primary scheme document: direct NCDC infrastructure funding is up to 70% loan
with at least 30% society share; the State Government route and any dovetailed
subsidy can change the applicable pattern proportionately.

2024 compendium: the loan period is 8 years including a 1–2 year principal
moratorium, and NCDC provides a 1% reduction in the applicable term-loan rate
for women-majority borrower cooperatives that repay on time.

Current NCDC FAQ: eligible societies may apply through the relevant State
Government or directly to NCDC with a DPR and prescribed information; the FAQ
states that project term loans are generally 8 years, margin money 5 years and
working capital up to 3 years.

Current NCDC common-loan route: the applicant must select the activity/purpose
and loan type, download the applicable form, complete it and submit it with
supporting documents through the NCDC route.

## Current facts for structured review

- Eligibility is limited to a cooperative society registered under a State or
  Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act with suitable bye-law provisions.
- Covered activity groups include hospitals and healthcare infrastructure,
  AYUSH, medical/paramedical education, diagnostics, insurance, telemedicine,
  digital health, margin money and working capital.
- Infrastructure funding may be routed directly or through a State Government;
  the record must keep loan, society contribution, share capital, subsidy and
  grant values separate.
- Direct infrastructure support is described as up to 70% loan with at least
  30% society share. The State route has different proportions and must be
  checked for the specific proposal.
- A qualifying women-majority cooperative may receive a 1% term-loan interest
  reduction for the loan tenure, subject to timely repayment and current NCDC
  rates/circulars.
- Security, appraisal, statutory permissions, repayment, monitoring and any
  subsidy convergence are conditional. Sanction is not guaranteed.
- The current NCDC application route is a loan-application handoff; it does not
  establish an automatic online approval or a current scheme-specific deadline.

## Editorial boundaries

Keep this record as a rolling financing framework, but require current NCDC
form/rate/channel confirmation before showing an actionable handoff. Do not
recommend it to a private company, LLP or individual without an eligible
cooperative applicant. Do not add loan support and any converged subsidy into a
single value. Preserve the women-majority interest reduction as a conditional
loan-rate incentive, not a grant.
