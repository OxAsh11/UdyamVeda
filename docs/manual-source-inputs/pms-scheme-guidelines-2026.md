## Source anchor

Trade fairs, exhibitions and participation assistance.

## Scope and eligibility

The guideline describes PMS as market-access and capacity-building support for
micro and small enterprises. Its scope includes national and international
trade fairs, exhibitions and MSME expos, vendor-development activity, modern
packaging, bar-code adoption, e-commerce, workshops and seminars, and retail
outlet support.

The current Development Commissioner page states that manufacturing and
service-sector MSEs with a valid Udyam Registration certificate are eligible,
subject to the applicable component and event conditions. Implementing routes
include DC (MSME) field organisations and other approved government agencies.

## Assistance and component boundaries

For individual MSE participation in trade fairs, the current official page
describes 80% subsidy on built-up space rent for general-category units and
100% for SC/ST, women, persons with disabilities, North-Eastern Region and
aspirational-district units, within the applicable event and stall limits.
The guideline also contains distinct assistance for bar-code adoption and
other marketing or capacity-building components. These percentages and caps
must be evaluated by component, beneficiary category and event; they are not a
universal cash entitlement.

The current event route exposes a separate PMS portal. The DCMSME circular
page lists 2026 operational notices, including restrictions on event counts,
document requirements and proposals for FY 2026–27. The current PMS notice
also says organizers may submit proposals for events from October 2026 through
the new PMS portal, subject to the extant guidelines.

## Application, window and fine print

The current online handoff is:
https://pms.dcmsme.gov.in/view-all-event

Applications and reimbursement claims are event-specific. The applicant must
verify the current event notice, valid Udyam status, selection/allotment,
document and invoice requirements, and any limit on supported events before
applying. Current notices must not be inferred from the historical guideline
alone. The separate IITF 2026 record carries its own 1 September–20 September
2026 window and 31 August 2026 Udyam cut-off; those dates are not copied into
the generic PMS record.
