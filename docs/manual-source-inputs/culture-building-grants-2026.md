# Ministry of Culture — Building Grants including Studio Theatres

Manual source capture prepared from the official Ministry of Culture scheme page, revised guideline PDF and application form.

- The scheme supports appropriately equipped training, rehearsal and performance spaces for artistes, including auditoria, open-air theatres, concert halls, rehearsal halls, training centres and studio/experimental theatres.
- Eligible applicants include not-for-profit cultural organizations with at least three years of cultural activity and registration, government-sponsored performing-arts bodies, and dedicated performing-arts university departments or colleges, subject to the stated conditions.
- Religious institutions, public libraries, museums, schools, non-dedicated colleges and non-performing-arts university departments are excluded. A previous building-grant project generally blocks a second grant before completion.
- For metro cities, new construction or purchase of built-up space is capped at INR 50 lakh and other projects at INR 25 lakh. For non-metro cities all projects are capped at INR 25 lakh. Assistance is normally limited to 60% of approved cost; NER projects may receive up to 90% within the same ceilings.
- The balance is matching share. Title/building evidence, approved local plans, architect-certified cost estimates, three years of accounts, matching-share evidence, recommendation, board resolution, bond and bank authorization are required.
- The application form states that the scheme remains open throughout the year, with a cut-off notified before Expert Committee meetings. Applications are routed to West Zone Cultural Centre, Udaipur through the prescribed postal route.
- Grant is released in two equal 50% instalments. The second instalment requires progress and professional certifications, utilization evidence, matching-share certification and physical inspection. The project must be completed within three years of the first instalment.
