# Ministry of Culture: National Presence grant-in-aid call 2026-27

Official Ministry of Culture call notice dated 27 May 2026 for the scheme component “Financial Assistance to Cultural Organizations with National Presence” for financial year 2026-27.

The objective is to provide financial assistance to cultural organizations with national presence for expenditure on cultural programmes and festivals during FY 2026-27. The notice states that assistance is restricted to 67 percent of the total project or event cost and directs applicants to the Ministry scheme page for the eligibility criteria, prescribed application format and required documents.

Eligible cultural organizations must use the prescribed format and include all required enclosures. Applications in another format or without the required documents will not be considered. Each page and enclosure must be signed and stamped by the authorized signatory, and two copies of the application or proposal must be submitted in separate file covers with page numbering and an index.

The notice required complete applications by 15 July 2026 at 5:00 PM at the address of the Eastern Zonal Cultural Centre in Kolkata. Applications received after that deadline, or before 1 June 2026, would not be considered. Canvassing is a disqualification. The FY 2026-27 call is therefore closed for the current date; a future call must be verified separately.
