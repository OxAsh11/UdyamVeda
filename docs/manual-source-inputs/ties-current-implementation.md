# TIES current implementation — manual official capture

The official Department of Commerce parliamentary answer describes the objectives and scope of the Trade Infrastructure for Export Scheme (TIES): improving export competitiveness, bridging export-infrastructure gaps, creating focused export infrastructure, supporting first-mile and last-mile connectivity, and addressing quality and certification measures. It identifies eligible implementing agencies as Central and State agencies and joint ventures with strong export linkages, with proposals placed before the Empowered Committee for grant release.

The answer reports implementation from 2017-18 and states that 66 projects in 21 States/UTs had been sanctioned through the date of the answer. This is current implementation context, not proof of a fresh FY 2026-27 application window. The original guideline's objectives, scope, eligible implementing agencies and extent of financial assistance, including the project-level ceiling and agency route, still require comparison and reviewer approval.
