# National Livestock Mission — official evidence capture

The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD) official National
Livestock Mission page was checked on 8 September 2026. It describes NLM as a
Centrally Sponsored Scheme and links the February 2026 amended guideline PDF
and the official NLM EDP dashboard.

## Current component and entrepreneurship evidence

Source anchor: current component guidelines; entrepreneurship assistance;
eligible entities; follow-up and exclusions.

The DAHD page identifies three sub-missions: Breed Development of Livestock
and Poultry; Feed and Fodder Development; and Research and Development,
Livestock Insurance, Extension and Innovation.

The entrepreneurship activities named on the page include rural poultry,
small ruminants (sheep and goat), piggery, and feed-and-fodder enterprises.
The page also identifies individual entrepreneurs, FPOs, SHGs and Section 8
companies as beneficiary/entity categories for entrepreneurship development,
with separate infrastructure and State Government activities.

The February 2026 revised guideline search extract records additional
breed-development focus for camel, donkey, horse and mule activities. The
record must therefore choose the applicable current component before applying
any benefit rule.

## Assistance and implementation evidence

The revised guideline search extract records a one-time 50% capital subsidy
of total project cost, capped at Rs.50 lakh for each unit, released in two
equal instalments. The first instalment is tied to bank or financial-institution
loan release and State Implementing Agency confirmation; the second is tied to
project completion and State Implementing Agency certification. The extract
also describes a bank appraisal and scheduled-bank guarantee route for
self-financing projects.

These values are treated as component-level evidence, not a universal NLM
entitlement. The final record must reconcile the applicable activity, entity
category, project-cost basis, financing or self-financing route, milestone
verification, exclusions, and current State expression-of-interest window.

## Eligibility, follow-up and exclusions

The official route requires an eligible NLM entrepreneurship activity, current
component conditions, State Implementing Agency processing and the official
NLM dashboard handoff. Follow-up includes expenditure or completion
verification by the State Implementing Agency and ongoing scheme monitoring.
The record keeps working capital, land purchase or lease, personal vehicles,
and component-excluded office accommodation outside the eligible benefit
unless a current component guideline expressly says otherwise.

No universal deadline is inferred from the DAHD page. State and component
availability must be checked before UdyamVeda displays an actionable window.
