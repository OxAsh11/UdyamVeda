# Market Access Initiative 2021 — manual official capture

The Department of Commerce operational guidelines state that the Market Access Initiative 2021 revision applied from 1 April 2021 to 31 March 2026. The main focus is export promotion and market development through approved projects. Eligible agencies and implementing agencies can include Government departments and organisations, Export Promotion Councils, registered trade-promotion organisations, Commodity Boards, recognised apex trade bodies, clusters, institutions and other categories specified by the scheme.

The components cover market research, capacity building, branding, standards and compliance, packaging, trade fairs, buyer-seller activity and related export-development work. Assistance is project and agency led; the scheme period, components, ceilings, approval route and any successor or consolidated Export Promotion Mission route must be checked before publication. This capture records the main focus, eligible agencies and components without treating MAI as an open direct grant for every exporter.
