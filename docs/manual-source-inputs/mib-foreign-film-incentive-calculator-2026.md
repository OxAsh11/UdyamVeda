# Manual official-source capture: India Cine Hub incentive calculator

Inspected: 08 September 2026

This capture records the current official India Cine Hub page used for the review-gated seed record. The page states that an online application requires a non-refundable processing fee in INR equivalent to USD 225, and that the current route does not accept documentary films, audio-visual commercials or music videos. It describes script evaluation by a Ministry-constituted expert, a normal permission-processing time of no more than three weeks after requisite documents, and required inputs including script/synopsis, crew passport details, Indian locations and shooting period, temporary-import equipment, applicable no-objection material and an official co-production agreement where relevant.

The page labels its tool “Incentive Calculator”, says users may be eligible to save up to 40% on spend in India, and asks about Significant Indian Content and the 15% or more Indian cast/crew bonus. The page displayed “Last Updated: 04-09-2026” when inspected.

This is a partial page capture. It does not replace the governing incentive guideline, which remains separately registered and requires direct revision/cap/claim verification before publication.
