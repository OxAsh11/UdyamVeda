# E-YUVA — BIRAC student and researcher innovation programme

Manual capture prepared from the official BIRAC E-YUVA page, scheme guidelines, historical call page and proposal guide on 8 September 2026.

The E-YUVA scheme supports applied and need-oriented entrepreneurial innovation among students and researchers through separate Innovation Fellow and E-YUVA Fellow categories. Innovation Fellows are for post-graduates and above; E-YUVA Fellows are undergraduate students. E-YUVA Centres provide pre-incubation space, equipment, workshops and mentoring with BioNEST Knowledge Partners. The programme covers biotechnology, healthcare, lifesciences, diagnostics, medical devices, drugs, vaccines, agriculture, industrial biotechnology, bioinformatics, waste management, sanitation, clean energy and relevant AI/IoT applications.

The official guideline describes separate support: Innovation Fellows receive category-specific monthly fellowship and annual research grant, while E-YUVA Fellows receive a quarterly stipend per student and an annual team research grant. Undergraduate teams may contain up to five Indian students, require a mentor or guide, and apply through the relevant E-Cell, Institutional Innovation Council or incubation-centre route. The guideline also contains degree-age, full-time, citizenship, duration, milestone, IP and reporting conditions.
