# Antariksh Venture Capital Fund (AVCF)

Manual capture prepared from the official SIDBI Venture Capital Limited fund-manager page and cross-checked against Department of Space/PIB releases on 9 September 2026.

## Fund overview

Antariksh Venture Capital Fund is a SEBI-registered Category II Alternative Investment Fund. The key investor is the Indian National Space Promotion and Authorization Centre (IN-SPACe), Department of Space, Government of India. SIDBI Venture Capital Limited is the fund manager and sponsor. The page lists SEBI registration number IN/AIF2/25-26/1942.

## Key investment criteria

The fund is looking to invest exclusively in Indian companies operating in the Space sector with Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of 4 and above. Early-stage companies may not yet be profitable, but financial stability, prudent resource management and a clear path to sustainable growth are described as important. Strong revenue projections, demonstrable cost efficiencies, a roadmap for milestones and a reasonable exit path are relevant to investment evaluation.

## Mode of investment

The fund would invest primarily through privately negotiated equity, convertible instruments and/or other permissible instruments in unlisted Indian space companies. The fund will seek standard venture-capital rights as an investor. Investment is subject to the fund documents, Private Placement Memorandum, applicable SEBI Alternative Investment Fund Regulations and negotiated diligence and investment terms.

## Contact and application route

The fund-manager page provides the SIDBI Venture Capital Limited contact route and an application form for interested companies. It does not state a universal grant ceiling, guaranteed ticket size, application deadline, or automatic selection. Applicants must verify current availability, fund documents and the requested diligence package with the fund manager.

## Cross-check status

The Department of Space/PIB operationalisation release reports an initial close with a committed corpus of INR 1,005 crore and identifies SIDBI Venture Capital Limited as investment manager. A later Department of Space/PIB reply states that the fund is in operation and that its Investment Committee has selected three startups for investment. These fund-level figures are not individual applicant benefits.
