# SITARE — BIRAC student innovation programme

Manual capture prepared from the official BIRAC SITARE page, scheme guidelines, current calls index and proposal guide on 8 September 2026.

SITARE supports student innovation in biotechnology through separate components. SITARE-GYTI is the translational research route for eligible Indian PhD or Masters students with institutional identity and the prescribed NoC. The official programme page describes up to 15 projects receiving up to INR 15 lakh each, generally for up to 24 months, subject to selection, milestones and an active Call for Proposals. BIIS is a separate undergraduate training and mentoring route. SITARE-Appreciation is a separate award route for selected BIIS work, described as up to INR 1 lakh. These figures are component maxima and must not be combined into a company grant.

The annual August/September timing described by BIRAC does not establish a current opening. The current BIRAC calls index was checked and no live SITARE call was verified on 8 September 2026. Keep the structured record paused until the current call, dates, route, student definitions, NoC, proposal terms, milestones, IP, reporting and agreement requirements are reviewed and approved.
