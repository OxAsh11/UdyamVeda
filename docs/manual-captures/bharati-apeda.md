## Editorial interpretation

BHARATI is represented as a closed, review-gated accelerator opportunity. The record must not describe it as a grant, loan, subsidy, guaranteed investor connection or automatic route to another Government scheme. A future cohort, application window, category form, RCMC timing and terms must be verified before recommending action.
