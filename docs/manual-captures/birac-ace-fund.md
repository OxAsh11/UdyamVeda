# BIRAC AcE Fund capture note

The primary source is the official BIRAC AcE Fund guideline. The current BIRAC programme page, FAQ and AIF application form are supporting official sources.

- AcE is a fund of funds promoted by DBT through BIRAC.
- BIRAC partners with SEBI-registered Category I or II AIFs (daughter funds).
- Daughter funds invest in biotech and life-science startups and SMEs; the current page lists healthcare, life sciences, pharma, biopharma, med-tech, diagnostics, industrial biotech, agri-tech and bio-IT/services.
- The guideline permits BIRAC commitment up to INR 30 crore or 30% of each daughter-fund corpus, and describes assistance to an individual startup up to INR 7 crore against partner-held equity.
- The partner must commit at least twice BIRAC's commitment to biotech/life-science startups over the daughter-fund term.
- The official FAQ separately limits countable follow-on investment and clarifies that the AIF application form is for funds requesting BIRAC contribution.
- No universal current startup call or deadline was verified; the record remains paused and review-gated.
