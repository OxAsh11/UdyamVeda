# Chips to Startup (C2S) Programme — official-source capture notes

Captured for local editorial review on 8 September 2026 from the official Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology C2S portal and linked official pages.

## Programme and applicant scope

The official C2S programme page describes an umbrella programme for specialised manpower in VLSI and embedded-system design, reusable IP, application-oriented Systems/ASICs/FPGAs and deployment through academia/R&D organisations and Startups/MSMEs.

The programme page states that Startups and MSMEs may participate through Academia-Industry Collaborative Projects, Grand Challenge/Hackathons/RFPs for System/SoC/IP Core development. It says project proposals may be submitted by Academia/R&D Organisations/Startups/MSMEs in notified project categories and that proposals are evaluated before financial support is considered.

The current official portal also exposes separate resources and calls. The BLDC Motor Controller ASIC page states that proposal submission is closed. The Regional Support Centre page records a 22 July 2026 submission deadline. These component-specific states do not establish an open general C2S call.

## Official guideline boundaries

The official guidelines require a prescribed proposal, project category and TRL, technical novelty and deliverables, target technology node, testing/fabrication/prototyping plan, commercialization or technology-transfer roadmap, resource requirements, collaboration roles, financial requirements and quarterly timelines as applicable.

The guidelines state that remote EDA-tool access and fabrication support may be provided, that milestone-linked fund releases apply to approved projects, that no separate funds are provided for creation of new facilities under the general programme, and that IP sharing follows agreed collaborator terms.

The official eligibility page defines Startups and MSMEs by the extant DPIIT or Ministry of MSME norms. Academic and R&D applicants have separate infrastructure, faculty, accreditation and proposal conditions.

## Editorial conclusion

C2S must be represented as a paused, review-gated umbrella opportunity. Project financial support, EDA access, fabrication support, training and institutional infrastructure are separate benefit buckets. No generic applicant ceiling or current open general application window is asserted. Human review must verify the applicable current call, category, deadline, benefit terms, contribution, milestones, reporting, IP and submission route before publication.
