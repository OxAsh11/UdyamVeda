MINISTRY OF ELECTRONICS AND INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
NOTIFICATION
New Delhi, the 21st December, 2021
Subject: Design Linked Incentive (DLI) Scheme

2. Objectives

The Design Linked Incentive Scheme shall offer financial incentives as well as design infrastructure support across various stages of development and deployment of semiconductor design for Integrated Circuits (ICs), Chipsets, System on Chips (SoCs), Systems and IP Cores and semiconductor linked design over a period of five years.

The objectives include nurturing domestic semiconductor-design companies, achieving indigenisation in semiconductor content and IPs, and strengthening design infrastructure through incubators while facilitating access for startups and MSMEs.

3. Eligibility

Financial incentives and design infrastructure support will be extended to domestic companies, startups and MSMEs engaged in semiconductor design for Integrated Circuits, Chipsets, System on Chips, Systems and IP Cores and semiconductor linked design.

A domestic company must satisfy the applicable resident-Indian ownership condition. MSMEs and startups are defined by the applicable Government norms. Approved applicants claiming incentives must retain domestic status for the required period after claiming incentives.

4. Tenure

Applications under the Scheme will initially be open for three years from 01.01.2022. Applications received under the Scheme will be appraised on an ongoing basis and implementation will continue as per approvals accorded under the Scheme.

5. Incentives

Product Design Linked Incentive: reimbursement of up to 50 percent of eligible expenditure subject to a ceiling of INR 15 crore per application for approved applicants engaged in eligible semiconductor design. Proposals should be capable of demonstration in an operational environment and ready for volume production.

Deployment Linked Incentive: incentive of 6 percent to 4 percent of net sales turnover over five years subject to a ceiling of INR 30 crore per application for approved applicants whose semiconductor design is deployed in electronic products.

6. Basis of computation

Eligible expenditure under the Product Design Linked Incentive includes design, development, testing, fabrication, validation, prototype development, product development and filing of intellectual-property rights, subject to the detailed guidelines. Net sales turnover for the Deployment Linked Incentive is assessed using returns filed before relevant taxation authorities and statutory-auditor certificates.

8. Design infrastructure

C-DAC will establish semiconductor design infrastructure, including a National EDA Tool Grid, an IP Core repository, hardware and software access, MPW prototyping, testing, validation and post-silicon support for startups and MSMEs.

9. Governance and implementation

The Scheme will be implemented through C-DAC. C-DAC will receive applications, verify applicant eligibility, conduct technical and financial appraisal, issue approvals, establish design infrastructure, examine claims and disburse fiscal support and incentives.

11. Disbursement

Fiscal support against the Product Design Linked Incentive will be released after approval and achievement of milestones in the approval letter. Deployment Linked Incentive claims may be submitted after the end of each financial year, subject to the applicable procedure and verification.

13. Scheme guidelines

Scheme guidelines are issued separately by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. The scheme and guidelines may be reviewed and amended periodically.
