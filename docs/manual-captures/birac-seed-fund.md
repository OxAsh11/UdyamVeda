# BIRAC SEED Fund manual source capture

The official BIRAC guideline describes the Sustainable Entrepreneurship and Enterprise Development (SEED) Fund for startups. It positions the fund as capital assistance for promising technology-driven startups through selected BIRAC-funded incubators, bridging promoter investment and angel or venture investment.

The guideline requires a selected incubator to have been supported through BioNEST, to have operated for at least three years with at least five resident biotech, med-tech or life-science startups, and to have in-house incubation, IP/technology-transfer and early-stage funding experience. BIRAC provides the incubator INR 100-200 lakh for implementation, to be used within 36 months from the implementation agreement.

The startup route is entity-based. The incubator selects Indian startups registered under the Companies Act, 2013, with at least 51% shareholders who are Indian citizens; OCI and PIO holdings do not satisfy the mandatory condition. The incubator may provide up to INR 30 lakh per startup through equity or equity-linked instruments for startup costs such as equipment, supplies and employees. A startup cannot receive BIRAC SEED funding more than once.

The instrument is held in the incubator's name under a legally binding agreement. The incubator must address conflicts of interest, include a BIRAC nominee in the governance committee, monitor milestones, submit half-yearly implementation reports and maintain an exit strategy. On exit, the guideline states that the incubator retains 50% of gross investment return and restitutes the remaining 50% to BIRAC within 60 days.

The current BIRAC page lists 16 SEED Fund partner BioNEST incubators and says startups can avail the fund through those partners. Its displayed impact figures are marked February 2023, and the reviewed official sources do not establish a universal current startup call or deadline. The opportunity is therefore paused and review-gated until a current partner-level intake is verified.
