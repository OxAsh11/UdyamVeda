# BIRAC LEAP Fund manual source capture

The official BIRAC LEAP Fund guideline describes the Launching Entrepreneurial Driven Affordable Products Fund for startups. It positions LEAP as an equity and equity-linked route for taking technology-driven startups from proof of concept toward piloting and commercialization through selected BioNEST incubators.

The guideline distinguishes LEAP from the existing SEED Fund. LEAP provides up to INR 1 crore per startup, while a selected LEAP partner incubator may receive up to INR 5 crore for implementation. The incubator must be a BIRAC BioNEST and SEED fund partner, have operated for at least five years with at least five resident biotech, med-tech or life-science startups, have incubation, IP/technology-transfer and early-stage funding capacity, and have at least four years of relevant funding or grant-management experience.

The startup route is entity-based. The incubator selects Indian startups registered under the Companies Act, 2013, with at least 51% shareholders who are Indian citizens; OCI and PIO holdings do not satisfy the mandatory condition. Funding may be disbursed in milestone-based tranches, and the equity or equity-linked instruments are held in the incubator's name under a legally binding agreement.

The guideline states that a startup cannot receive LEAP support if its SEED funding duration has not completed or if SEED was foreclosed or terminated. It also states that SEED, LEAP and AcE are mutually exclusive equity/equity-linked instruments and that support must not be concurrent. Startups suitable for up to INR 30 lakh are routed to SEED rather than LEAP.

The incubator must address conflicts of interest, include a BIRAC nominee in the governance committee, monitor milestones, provide hand-holding, submit half-yearly reports and maintain an exit process. On exit, the guideline states that the incubator retains 50% of gross investment return and restitutes the remaining 50% to BIRAC within 60 days.

The current BIRAC page lists six LEAP Fund partner BioNEST incubators and says startups can avail the fund through those partners. Its displayed impact figures are marked February 2023, and the reviewed official sources do not establish a universal current startup call or deadline. The opportunity is therefore paused and review-gated until a current partner-level intake is verified.
