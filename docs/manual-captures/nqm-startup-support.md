# National Quantum Mission startup-support evidence capture

## Primary source

- URL: https://dst.gov.in/sites/default/files/Guidelines%20to%20support%20start-ups.pdf
- Publisher: Department of Science and Technology, Government of India
- Capture purpose: confirm startup eligibility, application modes, funding/equity boundaries, mentoring, milestones and hub-mediated support

## Evidence anchors

### Eligibility

The guidelines require DPIIT registration at application or before release of funds, a product or service with market fit and commercialisation/scaling potential, technology use in the relevant solution or model, an Indian holding company, India-resident IP, majority Indian ownership and at least 51% Indian promoter shareholding.

### Application and selection

Applications may be cohort-based or rolling, may answer problem statements or open challenges, and may be recommended by a thematic hub or technical group. The evaluation considers need, feasibility, impact, novelty/IP, team, fund utilisation, funds raised and presentation. Legal and financial diligence, Board/IEMC approval and an equity agreement precede release.

### Funding and support

The guideline describes Track 1 seed funding up to INR 5 crore, Slab A up to INR 1 crore, Slab B above INR 1 crore up to INR 5 crore, and Track 2 Slab C equity or CCPS up to INR 25 crore. It also describes incubation-equity limits, milestone-based tranches, mentoring, facilities and Government-support possibilities. These are reference structures, not guaranteed applicant entitlements.

### Current route

The official DST startup call lists four hubs and instructs interested startups to apply to only one: quantum computing, communication, sensing/metrology, and materials/devices. Current hub-specific openings and terms must be confirmed before actioning.
