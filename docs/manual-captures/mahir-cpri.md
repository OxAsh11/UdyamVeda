# MAHIR manual source capture

The official Central Power Research Institute page describes MAHIR as a joint Ministry of Power and Ministry of New and Renewable Energy mission for indigenous development, demonstration and commercialization of advanced power-sector technologies. It identifies startup pilot support as an objective and lists carbon capture, green hydrogen/fuel cells, automation and AI, geothermal energy, alternatives to lithium-ion storage and other power technologies as focus areas.

The page marks the first call for proposals as closed and says a second call will be advertised soon. The first-call PDF sets the submission deadline at 20 January 2024 and invites academia, industry, utilities, startups and R&D institutions. The Ministry of Power Office Memorandum establishes a five-year mission from 2023-24 to 2027-28, committee-based approval, outcome-linked funding and Government/research-agency IP sharing. No current open call or universal startup funding ceiling was verified.
