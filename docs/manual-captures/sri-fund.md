# Self-Reliant India (SRI) Fund — official evidence capture

## Primary source

- URL: https://www.nvcfl.co.in/Scheme
- Publisher: NSIC Venture Capital Fund Limited (NVCFL), the official implementing/fund route
- Capture purpose: confirm the fund-of-funds structure, MSME boundary, daughter-fund route, exclusions and current application separation

## Evidence anchors

### Scheme identity and structure

The official NVCFL scheme page describes SRI as a Category-II Alternative Investment Fund. The structure is Government of India as anchor investor, an SRI mother fund, Category-I/II daughter funds empanelled with SRI, and onward growth capital for MSMEs through equity or quasi-equity.

### Eligibility and exclusions

The page states that MSMEs defined under the MSMED Act, as amended from time to time, are eligible for consideration. It separately excludes non-profit institutions, NBFCs, financial-inclusion entities, micro-credit entities, SHGs and other financial intermediaries. Industry focus and strategy remain subject to the Private Placement Memorandum.

### Applicant route

The NVCFL portal provides a daughter-fund interest route. That route is for prospective daughter-fund empanelment. An MSME must approach an empanelled daughter fund whose mandate fits the enterprise; SRI is not a direct government grant or direct founder loan application.

### Benefit boundary

The Ministry and official scheme material describe a Government provision of INR 10,000 crore intended to leverage approximately INR 50,000 crore through private participation. Those are fund-level programme figures, not money an individual enterprise can claim. Individual equity/quasi-equity terms, valuation, dilution, governance, exit, milestones and fees remain subject to the daughter fund, PPM and investment decision.
