# AgriSURE - Agri Fund for Start-ups & Rural Enterprises

Manual capture prepared from the official NABVENTURES fund-manager page and cross-checked against the NABARD FAQ, public notice and Ministry of Agriculture/PIB launch material on 9 September 2026.

## Fund overview

AgriSURE is a Category-II Alternative Investment Fund promoted by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare and NABARD. NABVENTURES Limited, a wholly owned subsidiary of NABARD, is the Investment Manager. The fund supports innovative, technology-driven, high-risk and high-impact activities in the agriculture and rural start-up ecosystem.

## Two separate schemes

The Fund-of-Funds (FoF) Scheme has a corpus of INR 450 crore and invests in eligible SEBI-registered Category I and Category II AIFs for onward investment in startups. The Direct Scheme has a corpus of INR 300 crore and aims to invest directly in early-stage startups focused on agriculture and rural-development opportunities. The total fund corpus is described as INR 750 crore.

## Target beneficiaries and sectors

Target beneficiaries include startups working in agriculture and rural development, including agritech, food processing, animal husbandry, fisheries, supply-chain management, farm mechanisation, biotechnology, waste management, renewable energy, agri-value chains, FPO support, farm-level technology and climate change.

## Application and fine print

Startups or AIFs seeking investment or contribution may reach out to the Investment Manager at agrisure@nabard.org through the official route. Meeting criteria does not guarantee sanction, contribution or investment. The investment decision is based on the merits of the case and the applicable fund documents. The official NABARD public notice says no money is charged for seeking assistance and warns against intermediaries collecting fees.

The fund duration is described as 10 years from inception, extendable by two years. The public page states that FoF investment in one AIF may be up to 5% of that AIF's corpus or INR 25 crore, whichever is lower. These are fund-level terms, not a startup grant, subsidy or guaranteed ticket.
