# PACE — Promoting Academic Research Conversion to Enterprise

Manual capture prepared from the official BIRAC PACE page, revised scheme document, historical RFP and proposal guide on 8 September 2026.

PACE supports academia to develop products and technologies up to proof of concept and validate them toward commercialization. It has two separate components: Academic Innovation Research (AIR), which supports academic PoC/prototype development with optional industry or LLP involvement, and Contract Research Scheme (CRS), which validates an academic process or prototype with a mandatory industrial partner or LLP. Academic institutes, universities, NGOs, research foundations and eligible research laboratories follow the primary-applicant route.

The current PACE page describes AIR projects with established proof-of-principle, up to 24 months and total project cost not exceeding INR 50 lakh, with non-recurring cost not exceeding 10%. CRS requires established PoC and validation-ready data; the guideline states grant support to academic and industrial partners with no ceiling in the reviewed document, so the active call and approved budget must control. IP, background IP, first-right, ownership, collaborator facility, milestone and reporting terms are component-specific.

BIRAC describes two regular call windows, 1 April–15 May and 1 September–15 October, plus possible need-based calls. The reviewed 2025 RFP is historical; no current AIR/CRS RFP or deadline was established on 8 September 2026. Keep the record paused until the active call and current terms are verified.
