# Official IN-SPACe Seed Fund Scheme — controlled manual capture

Indian National Space Promotion and Authorisation Centre (IN-SPACe) Seed Fund Scheme.

The official scheme document states that IN-SPACe formulated a seed fund scheme to encourage and provide seed capital to start-ups and micro and small enterprises in the commercial space sector. The scheme supports early-stage research and development of novel space technologies and space applications, including communication, navigation, earth observation, agriculture, weather forecasting, environmental monitoring, national security, scientific research, resource management, disaster management, urban planning, telemedicine and education.

Eligibility and applicant conditions include an eligible start-up or micro/small enterprise, technology as the core of the product or service, the stated Indian shareholding condition, promoter and angel investment matching the grant requested, and the stated limit on monetary support received under other Central or State Government schemes. Additional criteria may be decided by IN-SPACe.

Financial assistance is a grant of up to INR 1 crore for selected start-ups, released on a milestone basis in three or more instalments. The exact quantum and instalments are decided by the Experts Committee based on project progress. The first instalment may be up to 40 percent of the approved commitment. The grant should be fully utilized within three years from receipt of the first instalment.

The recipient must maintain proper accounts, submit fund utilization certificates, allow verification or audit, and submit a final report and audited utilization certificate. Intellectual property generated under the programme is stated to be owned by the start-up. An announcement of opportunities controls when applications can be submitted.

This capture is evidence for editorial review only. It does not establish that a new 2026 application window is open, does not approve the scheme record, and does not turn the INR 1 crore ceiling into a guaranteed applicant benefit.
