# GREAT manual source capture

The Ministry of Textiles' 13 February 2026 Rajya Sabha answer confirms that GREAT operates under the National Technical Textiles Mission and supports young innovators, scientists/technologists and startup ventures translating technical-textiles ideas into commercial technologies/products. It states maximum support of INR 50 lakh per approved startup, reports 24 approved startup projects, and describes a Review and Monitoring Committee.

The extracted eligibility criteria distinguish individual and startup routes. Individuals must be Indian citizens aged at least 21, use an incubator route, and cannot apply individually if they are promoters/shareholders of a technical-textiles startup. Startup applicants must generally be private limited companies, registered partnerships or LLPs, have turnover below INR 100 crore in any previous financial year, be no more than five years old, have at least 51% resident-Indian ownership/control, and have functional R&D capability or incubator association.

The PIB announcement adds the up-to-INR-50-lakh, up-to-18-month grant period, a 10% incubator allocation, and a minimum 10% incubatee contribution in two equal installments. The reviewed official sources do not state a current open application window or deadline, so this record remains paused and review-gated.
